# Commerce integration receiving and recovery
## Proposed workshare for Rigby

Prepared 13 October 2026 by Token Junkie Labs. Valid for scope discussion through 3 November 2026. Proposed fee: **USD 9,200**, split into USD 4,600 at written scope selection and USD 4,600 after the agreed receiving result. Planned effort: **10 active engineering business days** after access, original sources, reviewer and change windows are ready. This is an offer, not a customer order or a statement that an existing Rigby integration is faulty.

## Why this slice fits

Rigby's Septeo CNEE case describes its completed Medusa to Dynamics Sales CRM integration: email-first checkout matching, an ambiguity queue, and Account, Contact and Opportunity records. Its current Canso delivery model includes governed changes, staged migration/recovery and maintainable source ownership. We propose an implementation and receiving slice inside that existing delivery model.

The supplied contribution is a complete source-bound export comparison and original-case receiving tool. It retains both original exports, exact money precision, scope and mapping, duplicate/ambiguous findings, reviewer decisions and source hashes. A local browser supports inspection and review. The existing Merchant Support case writer receives the whole result under one original request; the existing full case package carries its history and source files. The contribution makes no remote customer, payment, order or CRM changes. Native filesystem/browser receiving remains pending in this source checkpoint; no customer data or acceptance is claimed.

## Paid delivery

Select one Medusa to Dynamics Sales CRM order flow for one customer, in one staging and one production environment, using the customer's existing integration and queue. We will receive its actual source, versions and export contract, map up to 12 agreed fields across the original order and Account/Contact/Opportunity records, implement a source-bound capture/reconciliation adapter, and complete one agreed integration recovery change where the baseline shows it is needed. The recovery change is limited to the selected order flow and its existing identity/upsert/ambiguity mechanism; it is not a replacement CRM or identity authority.

The implementation includes a reviewer-operated exception route, original-order same-request recovery, explicit evidence for amount/status/customer-key comparisons, a staged rollout with before-state and rollback instructions, and a complete independently reopenable delivery. Rigby remains prime contractor, customer and production decision owner. We work in the selected repository and follow its review and release process.

The integration may already meet the recovery requirements. In that case the paid work delivers the agreed capture/reconciliation adapter, original evidence and operational receiving without inventing a defect or adding a duplicate mechanism. If the baseline instead requires a broader change, we present the exact gap for written scope selection before implementing beyond this slice.

## Boundaries

Dynamics Sales CRM and Dynamics F&O are different targets. This offer selects Sales CRM. F&O product/stock/catalogue synchronization, Shopify subscriptions, payment processing, tax/accounting, bulk historical migration, a new identity service, 24-hour operations and extra integrations are outside this price. We preserve the existing payment and customer authorities. We do not infer that a matching email proves a customer identity or automatically resolve an ambiguous case.

A receiving export is complete only for its declared extraction scope. We retain original pages/cursors and selection evidence when the source adapter supplies them. Partial exports produce observed-only findings, not a claim that the counterpart system lost an order. Live integration health, settled payment and customer acceptance require their own original evidence.

## Receiving and payment

Rigby names one receiving owner and selects the acceptance rows before work starts. Day 5 presents the working source adapter and scoped receiving result; day 10 presents rollout/recovery evidence, complete source and a 75-minute handover. The fee includes one consolidated correction pass within the selected scope. Review is requested within three business days; silence is not acceptance. Access delays and unavailable release windows pause active-day counting. Additional scope or recurring support requires a separate written selection.

We retain the contribution and payment claim for work we create and can provide the implementation/source inventory and receiving record. We can provide recipient and payment details when the workshare is selected.
