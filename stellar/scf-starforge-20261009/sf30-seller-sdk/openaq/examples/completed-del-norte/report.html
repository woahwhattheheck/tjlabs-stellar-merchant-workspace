Method and interpretation

Separate each sensor, parameter and original unit. Archive intervals, source flags and per-observation coverage are absent and remain unknown. Counts describe retained observations rather than certifying complete monitoring. The mean, where selected, is an exact unweighted source-value expression.

If selected, an unweighted arithmetic mean of retained numeric values in one capture/sensor/parameter/unit/granularity/interval. The exact coefficient, power and divisor are supplied; no binary-float decimal replaces source data.

All reported numeric observations remain in numeric summaries; flagged and unknown-flag counts are shown separately.

Source coverage belongs to its individual observation or aggregate. It is not a completeness certificate for the selected project window.

Series remain separate by capture, station, sensor, parameter, unit, original granularity and interval. Overlapping windows and source editions are not combined or deduplicated.

Original source meaning

Original upstream-reported measurements; a timestamp is the exclusive period end, not necessarily an instant.

OpenAQ hourly averages; retain each reported period, flags and coverage.

Original numeric tokens are strings in the published table. No unit conversion, rounding, zero substitution or AQI calculation.

Every original row is retained. Repeated values and overlapping periods are not silently deduplicated.
