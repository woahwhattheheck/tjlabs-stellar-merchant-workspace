Accepted record vocabulary and money

Catalogue schedule: fixed or flexible. Registrations: active, cancelled or waitlist. Transactions: posted, pending or void. Confirmations: confirmed, declined or pending. Map source values explicitly; an unrecognized value remains an exception.

Amounts are unformatted decimal strings. The default currency is USD with two fractional digits. Fees and reported net payout never reduce a student’s registration balance. No payment, refund, email or provider import is executed.
