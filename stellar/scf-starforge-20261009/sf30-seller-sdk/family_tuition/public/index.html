Add or replace a household, student or other record

This creates a complete new source edition and preserves every other mapped row. A blank row ID selector creates a record; changing an existing identifier is refused. Payments record observed receipts and refunds only.

Vocabulary and exact money

Enrollment: active, cancelled, waitlisted. Receipt: posted, pending, void. Agreement: pending, signed, revoked. Staff confirmation: pending, confirmed, declined. A signed agreement must retain the complete document source and observed signer reference. Enrollment slot_ids is an array of explicitly selected slot identifiers. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD; named decisions and observed receipt times require timezone offsets.

Source amounts are decimal strings. All displayed minor units are exact integers. This application records observations and planning decisions; it does not charge a card, send email or alter a provider record.
