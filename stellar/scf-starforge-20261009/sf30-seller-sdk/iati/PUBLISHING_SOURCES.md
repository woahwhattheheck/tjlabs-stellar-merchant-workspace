## Receiving source policy

A receiving run must select a complete actual XML file with its original SHA, provenance and reporting-organisation reference. The supplied AidData 2016 and Transparency International examples in the maintained IATI product are different real sources with their own notices. Their historical/current distinctions must remain visible if used instead of a newly acquired FCDO file.

A locally authored engineering feedback row or correction example must be labelled as such. It is not PWYF-issued feedback or an original publisher statement. A real participant can supply its own source, official feedback and approved correction text through the same complete intake contract.

No source in this guide authorises publication to a customer's registry or use of customer credentials. The complete local release and original authorised publication process are separate recorded actions.
