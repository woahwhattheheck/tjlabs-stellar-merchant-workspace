The caller owns one native foreground and its ordinary disk directories. The provider, runtime builder, installer, source workspace, request journal and browser operation retain their original APIs.

The engineering fixture is explicit: the original public four-language HILA questionnaire is real, while the two response editions and numerical methodology are synthetic engineering inputs. A successful receiving result establishes the implemented workflow against those retained inputs. It does not establish official HILA scoring, a customer dataset, a survey finding, or permission to publish the questionnaire.
