Build this exact committed selection with:

~~~sh
node stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/distribution/build.mjs --repo /absolute/checkout --ref ORIGINAL_COMMIT --selection wqx-water-quality --out /absolute/wqx-release
~~~

Resume the same output with `--resume`. Use its actual archive and SHA-256 through the maintained runtime installer, selecting native dependencies plus `schema`, `openapi`, `mcp` and the original Linux/Python operator. Open the resulting READY installation in Runtime workspace → Applications → WQX water quality. Keep original projects and receiving files in the operator's private working directories.
