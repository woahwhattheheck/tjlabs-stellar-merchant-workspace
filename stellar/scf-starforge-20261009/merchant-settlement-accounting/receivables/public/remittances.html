1. Original remittance

CSV columns and matching

Use exactly these eleven headers. Header order may vary.

remittance_id,customer_reference,payment_reference,payment_method,payment_date,currency,scale,payment_amount,invoice_number,allocation_amount,allocation_date

Repeat one remittance ID and the full payment identity for its invoice lines. Match the exact saved customer reference and issued invoice number. All amounts are decimal strings at the chosen currency precision. Leave the final three columns empty to retain an unapplied receipt.

Existing matching receipts retain their original source. New payment records use the same operator-recorded receipt category as the receivables desk.
