# Interpretation and measurement

## Use the whole scheduled population

The original schedule identity is the denominator. Retain unavailable inventory, refused creative, no log, failed and cancelled observations. Unresolved schedule identity, parent or time makes the overall denominator unknown. Late-added obligations are separately visible. A planned future window is pending, not an observed success.

Campaigns, placements, assets, slots, venues and display windows have different units. Report each explicitly. Distinct venues determine a location bundle. Multiple screens at one venue cannot increase the venue count.

## Separate permission from actual display

An actual booking is a reservation decision over future source-supported terms. Live delivery adds installation, operating state and exact creative approval at the display instant. Later authority expiry does not erase an earlier valid signature; later host termination is a separate event. A later approval cannot authorise a prior unsupported display.

Distinct physical plays need actual unique external event references. Copied rows are one event. Conflicting logs remain unresolved. Player acknowledgements are separate technical observations. A completed suppressed/overridden ad mode is not a delivered visible ad.

Ad-visible runtime uses interval unions on each asset. For example, intervals [0,15),[10,25),[25,30) cover 30 seconds, not 45. Asset operating time is a different measure. This calculation does not turn elapsed operating hours into impressions.

## Keep estimates and responses honest

Each supplied estimate retains its actual method, source inputs and period. The desk does not add overlapping estimates or infer unique viewers from plays or traffic. Enquiry, click or booked response observations remain source-attributed responses; simple observation does not establish causal lift.

The current operating result and each immutable report edition are separate. Report R1 is assessed using what its inputs made available at R1's generation. Later R2 may have more evidence without repairing R1 retrospectively.

## Observe actual cash

Issued invoices reconcile exact signed lines in the declared currency. Clearing, refund, reversal, pending, failed payment and credit are distinct observed events. A repeated actual external transaction reference must reconcile globally, including across invoices. A conflict suppresses affected net receipts rather than selecting a favourable duplicate.

No automatic foreign-exchange conversion, renewal, refund, credit or make-good is generated. Preserve actual signed agreement and actual accounting outcomes. Partial cash cannot satisfy an unpaid panel or other declared installation prerequisite.

## Count all recurring work

The pair uses the same cohort_key, equal period durations and every required recurring phase in both methods. Period labels alone do not establish matching work; source notes must identify the real comparable population. Disjoint work_key values represent actual separate work, not separate files.

Active person-minutes are distinct from elapsed waiting. Explicit observed zero is a row; missing phase data is unknown. Current minus baseline is signed. Negative means less observed work; positive means more. All-current work is separate from the paired subset and remains unknown if duplicated or invalid work prevents a complete total.

The authored example has two pairs: baseline 60 and current 52 person-minutes, signed change −8. Its review phase increases while other work falls. These values are fictional fixtures illustrating the calculation, not observed customer savings. No wage/cash saving, annual extrapolation or causal effect follows automatically.
