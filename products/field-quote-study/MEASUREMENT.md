# Interpreting the field quote study

## Complete population

Define a completed visit-open interval, company/service scope and complete inventory original before interpreting fractions. Every eligible visit remains included even if the output fails, abstains, is unfinished or is later withdrawn. Known source-backed exclusions remain visible. Unknown identity, company or selection makes the complete denominator unresolved.

The report keeps publication, actual delivery, delivery before departure, authorised first delivery and the combined traceable/authorised/timely result separate. The denominator is the complete eligible visit population when that inventory is resolved.

The first actual departure defines the before-departure boundary. The earliest observed delivery is selected before evaluating whether its edition, chronology, input trace or authority is valid. An earlier problematic event cannot be replaced with a favourable later correction. Tied first deliveries of different editions remain ambiguous.

## Source trace and actual approval

The study evaluates actual supplied outputs and their input histories. It does not infer that an AI generated a finding correctly. Exact original bytes, receiving stage, recipient, use time and input cutoff must agree. A local recording cannot stand in for a later server receipt.

A matching approval is evidence about that exact edition and payload. The actual delivery event still needs valid actor, source and chronology. The report can retain an approved payload while the particular delivery remains unauthorised or unresolved. This separation is deliberate.

The company policy determines whether an actual delegated send is permitted. An actual Send now decision alone is insufficient without matching source authority. A locked decision does not count as authorised. Later explicit review or revocation is considered only when known at the relevant time.

## Exact line arithmetic

Each line computes quantity × unit price − explicit line discount with the declared half-up or half-even rounding to cents. Actual supplied line tax is added; line totals and edition totals must reconcile. The study checks arithmetic and source bindings, not the tax rate or technical adequacy of a service.

A price binding identifies the actual company/release/item/unit/currency rule and its known/effective interval. A differing unit price requires its actual source-backed override authority and reason. Unknown or future price evidence cannot backfill an earlier output.

The worked fixture's two units at 45.125 produce a net of 90.25; supplied tax is 9.75 and total is 100. The exact decimal values remain source strings and rational calculations. The rounding method is declared in the plan, not chosen after viewing the result.

## Whole recurring effort

Observe all active person-work incurred by the actual method. Required phases include capture, drafting, pricing, office review, correction and customer handoff; add real duplicate-entry or reconciliation phases when performed. Explicit observed zero is valid. Missing evidence is unknown.

Each person-work slice needs a canonical work_key and observer/time/phase identity. Renaming the source or row cannot make the same work disjoint. A paired visit requires complete, equal phase coverage for both methods. Extra phases participate in totals; mismatched coverage suppresses the paired difference.

The authored course has four paired visits: 160 existing-process minutes and 140 study minutes, a signed change of −20 and descriptive reduction of 12.5%. Office review rises from 30 to 40 minutes. An additional 30 unmatched study minutes makes 170 observed study minutes. The paired 12.5% is not extrapolated to all eight visits, annual capacity or cash savings.

## Worked outcomes

The complete authored eight-visit course contains five delivered quotes, three delivered before departure, four first deliveries with appropriate authority and two combining traceability, authorisation and timely delivery. One device original is known at 10:05 while its server receipt is at 10:35. The 10:20 server output remains unresolved; the 10:40 correction remains separately visible. An approval for an earlier quote version does not approve the changed delivered version.

These are authored method fixtures. Customer programme research describes potential study partners separately; it supplies no actual pilot outcome.
