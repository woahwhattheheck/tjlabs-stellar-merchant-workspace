# Field Quote Study Desk — complete domain implementation

This eighteen-family engine preserves original and returned CSVs, arbitrary mappings, exact original byte identity, binary documents, current and prior editions, complete portable sessions and digest-bound study review. It joins actual company, user, site and visit identities with evidence receipts, supplied quote editions and inputs, exact line calculations and price rules, actual authority and decision records, approval, delivery and customer observations, and complete person-time.

The calculation evaluates supplied events. It never conflates publication, sending, actual delivery, internal approval and customer acceptance. Each source input must have been received by the exact declared device/server/reviewer before actual use. Quotes retain their actual output status, first delivery and edition. Payload hashes bind approval to the actual quote document. Exact source prices and explicit authorised overrides remain distinct. Tax amounts are supplied observations. All eligible visits remain in the full population, including unfinished, abstained, failed and later withdrawn work. All recurring phases and unmatched work remain in the effort analysis.

This domain implementation includes the complete authored workflow and supplies the same calculation contract to the browser and native application. The domain's current exact fields and relationships are exported from core.cjs. Generic source is reusable; prospect material remains separate.

## Input and calculation contract

The canonical templates are the actual eighteen CSV contracts; the commercial programme's planning worksheets are separate. All fields are retained source text. An unmapped column remains null/unobserved rather than an observed empty field.

A study selects an original and returned edition for every family. Both identity and parent identity use explicit crosswalks. The independently selected evaluation side and UTC cutoff control workflow observations. A later consolidated source does not silently reconstruct an earlier cutoff. Complete consecutive visits, company/service scope, explicit exclusions and a retained inventory document establish the population. A withdrawn quote does not remove its eligible visit.

Each evidence record has actual capture/recording times and the SHA256 of linked original bytes. Separate receipts name device, server or reviewer stage, receiving identity, exact payload and availability/recording times. Quote inputs require availability at the exact receiving stage by actual use and the input cutoff. Local availability does not imply server availability. Quotes retain produced/recorded times, model and rule release, immutable quote/version identity, predecessor, actual status, document payload and itemised amounts.

The declared line rounding is exact decimal half-up or half-even to cents. Net is quantity times unit price minus explicit line discount, then rounded. Supplied tax is added and line totals reconcile to the supplied edition totals. Rate rules bind company, release, code, unit, currency and known/effective interval. A differing actual unit price requires its actual source-linked override authority and reason. Tax correctness and technical design are outside this arithmetic comparison.

Company approval binds the exact edition and payload available at actual review, effective approval authority, company/user/service scope, currency and amount ceiling. Later explicit supersession or revocation is considered only when known. Simultaneous independent active approvals remain unresolved. A delegated send requires an actual send_now decision, a company policy allowing delegation and matching sender authority at the event. A locked decision cannot be counted as authorised.

Actual publication, sent, delivered, withdrawn and voided records remain separate. The earliest actual delivery is selected before inspecting whether its payload, inputs or authority are valid. An invalid earlier delivery is not replaced with a favourable later correction. Tied deliveries of different editions remain ambiguous. The first actual departure is the before-departure cutoff. Customer viewing, acceptance, rejection, signature and cancellation bind their own exact edition/customer/payload and never stand in for an internal approval or a payment.

Every required recurring effort phase needs an explicit observed amount, including zero. Added phases participate in the complete totals. A work_key and observer/time/phase identify disjoint work; a renamed source or ID cannot manufacture another observation. Paired comparison requires the same complete phase coverage for each method. Unmatched and incomplete visits remain in the report, and total observed study effort remains separate from the paired difference.

## Authored operating course

The included course contains eight eligible visits, ten retained quote editions, five actual deliveries, three before departure and four first deliveries with matching approval. Two of eight combine traceability, authorisation and delivery before departure. A local original is available at 10:05 while its server receipt is 10:35; the 10:20 server output remains unresolved and the 10:40 corrected output stays separately visible. One delivered revision retains only its predecessor's approval and cannot count as authorised. Failed, abstained, withdrawn and unfinished work remains in the eight-visit population.

Four full paired visits contain 160 existing and 140 study person-minutes. Office review rises from 30 to 40 minutes. An additional 30 unmatched study minutes makes 170 total observed study minutes. The descriptive paired reduction is 12.5%; it is not extrapolated to all eight visits or annual cash.

All company names, people, outputs and approvals in this course are authored method fixtures. The source execution is reproducible through examples/build-course.cjs; the customer programme's actual public company facts remain in separate research files.
