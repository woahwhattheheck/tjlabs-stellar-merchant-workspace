Independent working comparison tool for maker-declared production and receiving.

Retain eight original tables, plan backward from a real production deadline, inspect resource reservations, and record what happened to each batch.

Actual ready pieces and projected output stay separate. Durations, yields, capacity units and compatibility groups are the maker's declared inputs. A clear capacity table supports a decision; it does not establish firing physics or authorize a kiln operation.
