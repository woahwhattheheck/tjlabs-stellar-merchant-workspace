# Interpreting a recurring-route study

## Define the question before comparing

A useful first study follows one actual team through a complete consecutive scheduled period. It asks whether the trial produces a more usable office proof and billing handoff, and what recurring work that requires. Existing systems remain the baseline. Agree the route/service scope, complete inventory, source owners, actual proof rules, observation window and review roles.

Do not choose only stops that already have attractive proof. Retain cancelled, blocked, missed, incomplete and no-attempt obligations. Revisit attempts remain attached to their original obligation. A known explicit exclusion needs a source-backed reason. An unresolved identity, time or selection suppresses the overall fraction.

## Four different observations

The report separates recorded service, complete device proof, a complete current office report and the actual current invoice-ready decision. These are different workflow outcomes. None is a universal statement that service happened or that money was received.

Actual report publication, sending, customer access and withdrawal appear as dated events. The complete report file remains available for inspection. A later correction does not rewrite the earlier report or its operational consequence.

Invoice headers, lines, accounting references and payment/refund observations form a separate chain. Missing payment observations produce unavailable net recorded receipts. An actual issued invoice remains retained even if its study source contains an unresolved link.

## Location and accuracy

A geolocation point describes a reported device position. Its acquisition time is separate from file capture and office sync. W3C's Geolocation API is source-agnostic; the reported accuracy radius does not guarantee actual location.

The desk measures WGS84 centre distance with Vincenty's inverse formula and compares the declared fence with the sum of reported position radius and declared site-coordinate uncertainty. Entirely inside, entirely outside, overlap and unknown remain separate. Nonconvergence or invalid coordinates gives unknown. The result is a geometric comparison, not a new confidence estimate.

Freshness is evaluated relative to recorded completion. A future point or a point older than the configured permitted age cannot satisfy the requirement. A valid geometric result still says nothing about the service work itself. A signature and image likewise need their actual operational context.

## Know what was available at each decision

A proof can exist on the device before it reaches the office. Use separate office receipt records and original payload hashes. A report must have its required inputs by its own generation time. Later arrival cannot retrospectively complete the earlier report. The authored course demonstrates an after-photo arriving at 12:00: the 09:20 report stays incomplete, while the 12:05 successor can use it.

Dispatch has a similar boundary. A later assignment, site-coordinate correction or requirement version cannot serve as evidence that the earlier technician had that information. Preserve the actual named editions and their recording/effective times.

## Observe all recurring work

Declare a complete phase set for both methods. The default includes dispatch, capture, service documentation, office review, correction, customer handoff and billing reconciliation. Add real recurring phases to the plan when the workflow needs them. Explicit measured zero counts as an observation; an absent phase does not.

Active person-minutes differ from elapsed clock time. Two people working for ten minutes produce twenty person-minutes; an unattended thirty-minute wait is not thirty active minutes. A canonical work key identifies one disjoint slice and must survive source-file renaming. Avoid importing the same work twice under different filenames.

Pair comparable actual observations with the same full phase coverage. Retain service kind, number of assets, access burden, crew arrangement and unusual exceptions as source context. A one-week pre/post or parallel study does not establish causality. Do not annualise one convenient route or convert observed capacity directly into payroll savings.

The authored course retains three paired stops at 84 existing and 72 study person-minutes. Office review rises while other phases fall. A fourth unmatched study stop contributes 24 minutes, so total observed study work is 96 minutes. The report preserves both the paired change and the whole observed workload.

## Review and next experiment

The operations reviewer examines schedule completeness, actual service statements, unresolved exceptions and proof. The billing reviewer examines the report and actual invoice/receipt chain. Their named decisions bind the full study source and configuration. A change makes earlier acceptance historical.

Use the first result to choose a concrete next operational experiment: improve office arrival of a specific payload, clarify an actual dispatch rule, remove an unnecessary duplicate handoff or resolve an identity mismatch. The application's output is the source-linked study and practical findings, with originals and exact limits available to the sponsor.
