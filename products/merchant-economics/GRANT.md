## Merchant economics, unfinished decisions and customer pricing

The merchant needs to turn its original operating activity and supplier costs into a price that it can explain, quote, deliver and maintain. That task often spans several sittings and several operators. Bills arrive at different times; an alternative may still lack a cost or volume; the customer may ask for a later price; and the receiving accountant needs the actual accepted source. The [complete Merchant economics product](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/products/merchant-economics/OPERATING.md) connects those stages in the existing private Pricing, Commercial and accounting workspaces.

The same [maintained pricing and economics source](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/tree/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf97-merchant-revenue-ops) supplies the browser, installed commands and original clients. The original financial owners retain their respective pricing, quotation, invoice, payment and accounting records. Each stage selects the complete preceding source and preserves its identity. The customer can keep an unfinished decision, compare an earlier original review, issue a separate accepted quotation and later reopen all of that work through the appropriate native receiving flow.

### Start from the actual source and cost period

The [Pricing workspace](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf97-merchant-revenue-ops/WORKSPACE.md) saves the original seller configuration, observation capture or retained history, selected route scope and explicit commercial assumptions. Its current working revision stays separate from older saved reviews. The operator records a unit quotation, quotation basis and intended monthly demand; original observed counts keep their actual period and counting meaning.

The [Supplier costs workspace](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf97-merchant-revenue-ops/economics/README.md) retains the exact original bill, credit note, quotation, usage capture, accounting report or supporting document. The operator maps the actual source columns, reviews each included or excluded row and preserves its reason. Every row retains its original source identity. Amounts, currencies, quantities and units remain explicit.

An allocation book binds the selected pricing review and period. Monetary source periods must match that book, and the operator records the period-alignment explanation. Every selected route receives a coverage assessment, including routes with no allocated line. The native engine conserves each exact signed amount across route allocations and the remaining amount. Actual bills and planning quotations remain separate views; unknown costs and missing denominators remain visible.

A reviewed eligible cost proposal continues into the original Pricing save with its expected revision and retained request. That operation changes the selected cost assumptions in a new pricing review. The operator uses the original repricing workflow when preparing a merchant atomic-price change, retaining the complete ordered payment choice, explicit asset valuation, unit scale and original release selection.

### Retain unfinished alternatives and recover the original save

The [unfinished-decision workflow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf97-merchant-revenue-ops/WORKING_COPIES.md) retains named working copies before an alternative is ready to calculate. Partial volume, quotation, cost or target text remains in the form with the original pricing source, selected scenario, service page, comparison name and earlier working history. The operator can reopen a named copy or any earlier retained revision and save a separate copy for another proposal.

The native predecessor check makes concurrent changes explicit. When two windows continue the same copy, one next revision is committed and the other form remains available for a separate copy or an explicit review of the newer original. An interrupted response retains its original request. The operator can also download the complete current raw inputs directly, or export the exact selected saved working revision.

Receiving a working package selects the same original pricing source and creates a new local copy identity. The complete original record and its receiving context remain available. The receiver needs the actual source files in its workspace; the full backup supplies those originals when the decision moves to another host.

When the inputs are ready, the [original Scenario planning engine](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf97-merchant-revenue-ops/PLANNING.md) evaluates up to twelve alternatives against one immutable review. The operator inspects normalized commercial assumptions, exact contribution results, available break-even or target suggestions and any unknown coverage. Applying a suggestion is an explicit edit followed by another calculation.

Before dispatching a finished-comparison save, the browser retains its exact source, normalized inputs, selected scenario, reviewed calculation hash and original request ID in the working history. Reopening recovers that same request after a lost response. An explicit original validation rejection returns the raw form to editing. Unknown outcomes retain the original body for recovery.

A saved comparison has a complete readable decision report alongside its Plan JSON, Comparison CSV and original terms. The report displays every saved baseline and alternative, original observed scope, exact totals, route results, targets and suggestions, changed assumptions and payment/source identities. It reads the retained calculation and its verified original source. A later working edit preserves that earlier finished decision.

### Compare a tariff and then review recorded use

The [Tariff planning product](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf97-merchant-revenue-ops/tariffs/README.md) compares graduated and volume tariffs at explicitly selected quantities. A graduated tariff totals the used bands; a volume tariff charges every request at the tier selected by the complete quantity. The review retains exact tier totals, effective unit-price bounds and threshold behavior.

A selected uniform quotation is a distinct result. Its upward rounding to six decimal places and the resulting gross difference stay visible. The operator reviews the original source, selected quantity and complete proposed change before the existing scenario and Pricing writers save their own request identities. Atomic payment amounts continue through the separate native repricing decision.

The [Performance workspace](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0c52fb12870b25cd057006f44ac93c0b10736a20/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf97-merchant-revenue-ops/performance/README.md) subsequently selects an actual retained usage version, period, count basis, original payment option or paired service source, and available supplier-cost edition. It compares the selected tariff and saved uniform quotation at that observed quantity. Original allowances, successful responses, consumed units and settled payment records retain their own meaning.

A future monthly proposal uses an explicitly entered planned quantity. Its source, horizon and review remain separate from the observed period. The retained proposal continues through the original tariff selection, scenario and Pricing requests. After an interruption, the operator reopens those same original requests.

### Hand the complete operation to the next maintainer

A handover selects the complete retained units appropriate to the customer's work:

| Retained unit | Continued customer work |
| --- | --- |
| Original Pricing and Supplier-costs backup | Saved pricing reviews and prepared decisions, original supplier files, cost editions, finished comparisons, unfinished working histories and saved performance operations. |
| Complete original tariff portfolio | Saved tariff definitions and selected quantities in the same restored Pricing workspace. |
| Commercial case package | Customer scope, quotation editions and responses, accepted work and full tariff/performance material. |
| Original accounting exports | The customer, invoices, agreement calendar, payments, credits and selected account records. |
| Readable decision and original calculation files | The exact selected commercial explanation, JSON, CSV and maintained merchant terms. |

The complete Pricing backup captures selected working histories before the pricing-source prefix that they reference. Its original restore reopens those private record chains in the selected receiving directory. The state-only CLI can then list, load, save, export or import the same unfinished work against that saved source.

The receiving operator opens the actual result, checks the selected customer, source, period, units and amounts, operates the needed controls and continues an explicit saved step. The original source, selected file and operating identity remain attached when moving between browser, installed command and customer package. The report and customer-facing views should be inspected at desktop and narrow widths, including exported and reopened output, as part of that same complete operation.
