# Distribution Lot and Order Study Desk

## Complete operating boundary

This source study follows a consecutive warehouse/material population across actual purchase lines, receipts, physical lots, transfers and same-material repacking, certificate review and QA holds, original customer order requirements, supplied allocation recommendations, named decisions, actual allocations/picks/dispatch, returns, write-offs and commercial postings. Its39 source families keep original bytes and every source revision. The maintained application opens empty and includes a complete fictional operating course.

## Physical stock and source identity

Use one immutable physical lot key. A supplier lot label alone is insufficient to distinguish physical lots or aliases. Products declare a material and physical base unit. Source-defined dated unit conversions are required for a different recorded unit; the app does not invent package sizes or conversions. Same-material repacking consumes exact input lots and creates exact outputs, with actual recorded process loss. Every movement retains its original transaction reference. Stock in transit and returned stock pending disposition remain separate from released stock. Actual over-receipt, shortage, over-pick or dispatch remains visible and unresolved quantities prevent complete reconciliation.

## Historical decisions

Source availability, actual event time and record time remain separate. A supplied recommendation binds its exact complete known order/lot/input population, policy edition and original output. A later certificate amendment or review cannot make an earlier allocation eligible. Expiry, retest and manufacture dates have different meanings. The applicable customer rule and promised delivery date govern the required remaining life. FEFO/FIFO ranks eligible physical stock only after actual QA release, hold, product/grade, warehouse, quantity, customer and date requirements are resolved.

A supplier certificate is retained independently from the operator's named review and lot-state decision. A released lot needs its exact review inputs and all actual relevant holds cleared under scoped authority. The application records and checks actual supplied operator decisions; it grants no physical release authority and makes no general regulatory certification claim.

## Outcomes and measured work

Complete denominators retain all observed receipts, lots and order lines, including cancelled, pending, partial and unresolved cases. Report order, line and unit fill separately. Actual accepted dispatch must remain distinct from an allocation or pick. Partial returns and actual return dispositions retain original dispatch lineage. Actual write-off quantity and posted monetary cost remain distinct from supplied risk scores or hypothetical avoided loss.

Both operating methods retain eight recurring phases per complete physical/order work member: source_capture,receiving_reconciliation,certificate_qa_review,allocation_planning,exception_approval,dispatch_reconciliation,outcome_follow_up,correction. Preserve increased work, incomplete comparisons and all current observed effort. Separate one-time setup. Do not add elapsed waiting time to active person-minutes or monetary postings.
