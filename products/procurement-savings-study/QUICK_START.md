# Operating the Procurement Savings Reconciliation Study Desk

## 1. Establish the consecutive study

Create a new study and enter its actual title, original and returned boundaries, source rights and named reviewer context. Choose one operator and, optionally, a site. Record the consecutive period and overall source cutoff. Keep the data class operational for actual originals; the supplied worked example is authored_course.

Define the initiative population from the whole tracker/source population before interpreting outcomes. Include initiatives with no realized amount, pending approvals, adverse results, cancellations and exceptions. Record expected member count and actual inclusion/exclusion basis.

## 2. Retain originals before mapping

In Supporting originals, retain each actual contract, baseline document, original transaction extraction, allocation instruction, credit, policy and Finance decision as exact bytes. Enter the document's original availability. Capture time is separately retained. Link it to the exact source or returned record.

In CSV intake & mapping, choose the family and side, import the exact source file and map every canonical field explicitly. Blank mapping means unobserved; a blank in an observed source column remains an observed empty string. Do not manufacture missing columns. Download a header template when preparing an authorized extraction.

A later retrospective CSV can contain original versions known earlier. Its complete-edition availability belongs to the CSV; each row's recorded_at/available_at and its supporting original document determine historical decision availability.

Import explicit empty families where the completed source inventory confirms none existed. A missing table and a confirmed empty table are different observations. Record each expected family count in Population & work plan.

## 3. Keep actual identities explicit

Retain supplier legal identity, item/specification and unit basis, operator/site, initiative, original source system, transaction reference and original line reference. Natural business IDs and immutable source-version IDs serve different purposes.

Identity maps pair original and returned records. Matching names do not establish identity. Unique exact-ID pairing is a convenience for explicit source/returned mapping, not authorization to combine economic transactions.

Multiple source aliases of the same economic purchase must have consistent economic values. Keep all original aliases. Actual purchase allocation is one account across those aliases.

## 4. Establish approved baseline and terms

Retain the actual historical-paid, contract, budget, market or opening-quote baseline and its comparable item, unit, specification and commercial terms. Add the exact complete baseline input set, then use the source-binding helper to retain each row hash and the review basis. Record the real Finance reviewer, time, decision and comparability.

A normalization requires an explicit supported scope adjustment. Do not silently alter tax, freight, pack size, payment terms or specification. Preserve the original approved forecast and compare it with the recomputed forecast.

Retain the actual source policy, effect method and treatment of recurring/one-time costs. Add actual approved exchange rates and exact currency treatments where required.

## 5. Reconcile actual purchases and allocations

Retain actual posted quantities, source unit prices and net item amounts. The amount remains the observed amount even when quantity multiplied by unit price differs.

For each mapping, retain the exact spend, supplier, item, initiative and term identities; bind the complete then-available mapping input set; and record the actual procurement decision. An accepted mapping supports an explicit quantity assignment.

Allocate a shared purchase by stated quantity. Keep its unassigned remainder. Attribute signed credits, returns, rebates or corrections explicitly to the relevant assignment. Do not invent a proportional split.

For costs, retain transaction_amount for the original whole posting, amount for this initiative's explicit allocation, allocation_key for that allocation, currency, kind, period and allocation evidence. The original transaction account must reconcile across every initiative.

## 6. Freeze the calculation and Finance decision

Create the calculation case for the exact initiative/item/period and cutoff. Retain every needed original input and its exact hash. Use the source-binding helper after the originals and referenced decisions have been retained.

Inspect the exact spend bridge, actual and constant-currency results, costs and avoidance. Keep calculated values distinct from the actual procurement submission. Bind the submission to its calculation basis.

Record the actual Finance decision against the exact submitted row. An adjusted approval retains both submitted and approved values. A correction to inputs or submission creates a new current edition; historical acceptance remains in history.

## 7. Compare complete work

Observe each of the eight work phases under both named methods for the same initiative members. Preserve actual signed active person-minutes, work identities and matching groups. Retain increased review and correction. Do not replace missing measurements with zero or omit an inconvenient member.

Use Initiatives & reconciliation to inspect the complete population, mappings, transaction accounts, signed adjustments, unresolved issues and work groups. Use Savings & Finance to inspect currency-separated calculations and decision history.

## 8. Review, correct and reopen

Return comparison shows all selected field and document differences. Source-bound review records named procurement and Finance review of the exact whole-study edition. This is separate from original business approvals.

Retain a sourced correction as an explicit new source edition. For a genuine new business version, retain the actual new immutable ID, stable record key, revision and superseded original; preserve earlier originals. Correct a source mapping without pretending it was a new business event.

Download the complete session and ZIP. Reopen the session on the receiving machine and inspect retained drafts, history, original bytes and reviews. A pending stale form remains recoverable, but it must be prepared against the current selected extract before applying it.
