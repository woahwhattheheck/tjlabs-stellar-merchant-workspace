# Complete observation and work comparison

Define the project and claim population before inspecting favourable results. Retain the original inventory, all consecutive eligible claims and actual milestone states. Do not replace an unresolved claim with a smaller denominator. The report separates retained, included, excluded, unresolved, prepared, submitted, certified and rejected claims.

Operations should check the physical work and source lineage; finance should check authority, pricing basis, period movement, retention, invoice totals and actual cash identity. Their study reviews bind the exact current source. A recorded acceptance is a review observation, not a payment certificate or permission to write into another system.

## Recurring work

Use one row for actual disjoint work by one person. A stable work_key follows that same work across exports. A renamed source file or new row ID does not create new labour. person_id, workflow, cohort_key, periods and actual start/end times remain explicit.

Record collection, measurement, transcription, approval chasing, correction, duplicate entry, finance reconciliation and recurring product feedback. The plan may use additional exact phase names. One-time setup has recurring=false and is reported separately.

Minutes are active person-minutes. Two people working for ten minutes produce twenty person-minutes, with separate rows. Elapsed waiting for a client, certification or payment is not active work. An explicit zero is a recorded observation; an absent phase remains unobserved.

The engine compares complete project/cohort pairs with both methods, equal period durations and every declared recurring phase. It preserves increased work: current minus baseline may be positive. It reports all current recurring work separately, including unmatched cohorts. Duplicate or overlapping work records suppress the affected total instead of inflating it.

Different work keys must represent disjoint work. Source names alone are not a disjointness argument. Keep the actual selection and aggregation explanation in measurement_notes.

## Interpretation

The authored course has 80 baseline and 64 current person-minutes, including approval time increasing from 10 to 15. Its signed change is -16 minutes for that complete authored cohort. This is arithmetic on fictional source observations, not an expected contractor saving.

For a real trial, record differences in project size, subcontracting, client approval requirements and work mix. A simple pre/post comparison does not establish causality. The desk reports observed change without converting freed capacity into cash or projecting a small sample over a year.

Claims, milestones, issued documents and receipts are different denominators and events. A receipt may pay part of an invoice; an invoice may be disputed; a milestone may be achieved before it is invoiced. Keep these distinctions when discussing the result with the operator.
