# Construction Progress Study Desk

Open **Construction_Progress_Study_Desk.html** in a current desktop browser. The application runs offline and starts empty. It accepts actual supplied records from the contractor and its accounting or site systems. No account, provider connection or automated payment action is required.

The desk follows two connected workflows:

- Accepted contract scope, measured work, variation instructions and approvals, actual claims, certification, retention, invoices and client cash.
- Supplier commitments, partial deliveries, actual supplier invoices, remaining uninvoiced commitments and supplier cash.

Measured, milestone, lump-sum and mixed contract terms are explicit. Certification, retention and deposits may be required, explicitly not applicable, or unresolved. No tax rate, retention percentage, payment schedule or statutory entitlement is supplied as a default.

## Start with one complete project population

1. In **Study scope**, identify the actual original and returned trial boundaries. Leave the source class operational for real records.
2. In **CSV intake & mapping**, import exact source files and map the 30 canonical families. A missing mapping remains unobserved. A blank cell in a mapped column is a distinct observed blank.
3. Retain the accepted agreements, measurement records, approval documents, certificates, invoice originals and bank evidence under **Supporting originals**. Link each original to its actual record.
4. In **Construction & measurement plan**, declare the decision boundary, the later-or-equal knowledge cutoff, complete claim population, actual currency precision and recurring work phases.
5. Review **Progress, claims & costs**, then the separate exact **Return comparison**.
6. Have operations and finance record their decisions against the current study digest. Editing the study preserves prior decisions but requires a new review of the changed edition.
7. Use **Save complete file** frequently. **Save & recover** exports the entire study, unfinished forms, branches, source originals and maintained application code.

## Worked course

The optional **Open complete authored workflow** action loads clearly labelled fictional records. It demonstrates two cumulative certificates with a true second-period movement, an unpriced instructed variation, separate signature/start/reception milestones, actual retention and deposit application, partial supplier fulfilment, historical report lineage and matched recurring work.

The authored numbers are examples of the calculation contract. They are not contractor performance observations.

## Included material

- OPERATOR.md — the ordinary editing and recovery workflow.
- SOURCE_INTAKE.md and DOMAIN_HEADERS.json — exact original/header/mapping contract.
- DOMAIN_CONTRACT.md — supported financial and chronological interpretation.
- MEASUREMENT.md — complete population, effort and review method.
- NATIVE.md — Node commands and source building.
- RECEIVING.md — actual operating-system and rendered-browser receiving course.
- DELIVERY.md — complete package contents and source identity.
- examples/ — complete authored source, input CSVs and readable report.
- verification/ — actual maintained source-course results.

The application observes and compares supplied records. Existing signed contracts, operational authority and accounting systems remain the source of actual approvals, invoices, certificates and cash.
