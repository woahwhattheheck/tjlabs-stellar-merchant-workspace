# Migration acceptance implementation workshare
## Prepared for Definian · 13 October 2026
**Proposed fee: USD 9,600 · Delivery: 15 active business days**

### A complete workstream for your delivery team
Definian's published migration approach includes repeatable conversion and reconciliation to the original data. We can take one implementation slice through native loading, full-population acceptance, correction and editable handover, while your team keeps the client relationship, business sign-off and production decisions.

The prepared application uses original dlt 1.31.0 and DuckDB. It retains source files and pipeline state, separates extraction, normalization and loading, and gives operators a browser, CLI and API for the same revisioned workspace. Acceptance compares every agreed business row and duplicate multiplicity, then adds keys, required fields, parent/child integrity, grouped counts and exact decimal/integer totals.

Measured findings remain immutable. Acknowledgement records review without clearing a failure. An accepted exception applies only to an explicitly reviewable finding in that exact report. Required identity, parity and native-completeness failures stay blocking. Source corrections produce new editions and fresh native verification.

### The proposed implementation scope
One customer workspace and one agreed migration slice, covering:

- Up to four CSV, JSON or NDJSON resources and all agreed named child-table mappings.
- Complete input register, independent expected values, explicit types, keys, NULL conventions and business rules.
- Original native extract/normalize/load, complete closed database and pipeline state, and fresh full target verification.
- Full typed parity and related-object acceptance, with attributed exceptions and one planned correction plus confirmation cycle.
- Reviewed retained consumer selection and rollback; complete source/runtime handover and independent fresh, forward, same and older receiving.
- Editable application source, operator guide, complete evidence files and a ten-business-day handover defect-correction window.

The proposed sizing envelope is 250,000 total business rows and 128 MiB of literal source input. These are quotation boundaries, not measured throughput claims. Day 1 confirms the complete normalized/export populations and host capacity. Wider populations, additional resources or changed semantics require an agreed scope adjustment.
