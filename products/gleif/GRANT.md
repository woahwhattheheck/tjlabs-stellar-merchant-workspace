# Company research, recurring service and customer receiving

The GLEIF company-research product gives a customer an editable company population, the original official records behind each decision, readable research, reusable data and a project another operator can continue. Open **Applications → GLEIF company research** or the installed **starforge gleif** command. The browser, CLI, typed Node client and nineteen merchant tools share the same project.

The complete [operating guide](./OPERATING.md), [source contract](../../stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf30-seller-sdk/gleif/SOURCE_CONTRACT.md) and [original Foundation example](../../stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf30-seller-sdk/gleif/examples/observed-foundation-20261012/README.md) supply the actual commands, schemas, original responses and ready receiving inputs.

## Useful customer task

A company-data team, software implementer or research provider selects a legal-name query or a known LEI population, records why each entity belongs in the customer's work, reviews later changes and hands over a complete usable edition. The proposed commercial unit is one agreed population and review period, one receiving route, and the full report/dataset/project package. A commissioning workorder names the population, source date, reviewer, destination, refresh cadence, turnaround and fee. Recurring work produces the next reviewed edition and a complete change explanation.

GLEIF provides its LEI access service free of charge and publishes the data under CC0. The service offered here is TJLabs' company research, source integration, operated refresh and receiving support. Original source attribution and terms travel with the delivery. See the [official API](https://www.gleif.org/en/lei-data/gleif-api/) and [LEI data terms](https://www.gleif.org/en/meta/lei-data-terms-of-use).

## Complete operating procedure

1. **Agree the decision and source selection.** Record the actual query or LEIs, country and status filters, receiving application, reviewer and acceptance criteria. Choose the installed source revision and a private saved project. Record the exact population returned by that query.
2. **Acquire complete original records.** Capture every returned page or import the already acquired full response envelopes. Preserve the original URL, acquisition receipt and source observation time. A later refresh becomes another source edition; an interrupted capture resumes its retained pages and next request.
3. **Review the companies.** Inspect legal name, LEI, separate entity and registration statuses, legal jurisdiction, legal and headquarters addresses, renewal date and original relationship references. Record inclusion, rationale, use case and the next assigned work. Individual and batch edits retain their source edition and opened review version.
4. **Resolve source or review changes.** Compare complete source editions and inspect changed fields and relationships. A reviewer can load the current saved decision or explicitly retain their draft against the displayed current version. The saved history makes earlier source-specific decisions available.
5. **Deliver the selected research.** Prepare the HTML report, full JSON, machine CSV, spreadsheet CSV and JSONL. Each report freezes its selected source and review editions. Prepare the same population as a native SF30 dataset and operate its declared filters, ordering and page cursor.
6. **Commission the selected merchant service.** Use that dataset's original description and files with the existing dataset seller. Configure the customer's actual host, route, source terms, payment network, asset, recipient, amount and facilitator. The original buyer approval, purchase, delivery and accounting interfaces govern the service.
7. **Receive and continue the project.** Export the complete original project, receive it in another private directory, reopen company details and original files, query its retained dataset, and save the next agreed review. The same source, review, report, dataset and operation histories remain available to the receiving operator.

## Customer acceptance and continuing support

The customer receives the agreed company population and review period, every selected original response, the retained inclusion rationale, all five report formats, the native dataset and the complete editable project. Acceptance covers the customer's actual use: open the selected company, trace it to its original source, inspect a source change, query the agreed dataset and continue a saved review after receiving.

Measure effort and elapsed time against the customer's preceding method for that same population and completion criterion. Record actual source acquisition, research review, customer correction and receiving effort separately. Each recurring delivery retains its observation date, original decision history and full output so the customer can use the current edition and revisit an earlier one.
