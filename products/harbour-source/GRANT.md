## Harbour source, operating evidence and complete customer receiving

### A complete source-side harbour workshare

Harbour administration connects people, vessels, places, applications, offers, agreements and visits. It also depends on original operating decisions, conditions, documents and financial records. Moving those records into a specialist platform requires more than a list of names: the original relationships, source dates, consent statements, currency and sign meanings, permissions and history must remain inspectable.

The installed Harbour Source Workspace provides that full preparation and receiving workflow. An operator retains complete original CSV and binary files, maps their actual columns and opaque identifiers, declares the whole selected population, inspects source findings, records attributed corrections, defines source-bound operating and financial profiles, reviews the exact selected state, prepares files against actual supplier templates and compares the supplier's observed return. The [installed operating guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/817559b68e176675498a8fae773e9dade1f111eb/products/harbour-source/OPERATING.md) joins the offline browser, original CLI, typed merchant Tools and the existing Workshare/Customer Installation.

The workshare preserves the roles of the source owner, harbour officers, finance owner, engineer and specialist supplier. Source owners provide authorised originals and their meaning. Engineers make mappings, evidence and unresolved differences reproducible. Harbour officers retain suitability, offers, permissions and operating decisions. Finance owners retain accounting policy and signoff. The supplier retains its licensed platform, sanctioned import route, production credentials and destination behavior. A file preparation or structural review does not confer those authorities.

Original PDFs, HTML, CSV and other binary evidence travel with the complete working record and handover. ### Operating evidence stays attached to its original source

An operating profile binds the exact saved scope, actual source evidence files, explicit role/field selectors, code meanings and operator explanation. It preserves the distinction between an initial enquiry, a complete application, an offer, agreement dates, consent and an officer's recorded decision. Date-only values and timestamps with offsets remain different source types. Suitability, allocation, waiting order, reinstatement or consent are not inferred from a row's presence.

The engine uses the declared evidence to report the source's operating relationships and unresolved conditions. Unknown codes, missing meanings, incomplete source coverage and incompatible values remain findings. A published procedure, a historical licence condition and a later operating instruction remain separate original editions; one does not silently overwrite another. ### Exact financial reconciliation with explicit boundaries

The financial profile requires an explicit amount representation and sign basis, original currency, account, namespace and period fields, permitted cross-namespace or cross-period relationships where applicable, and complete source evidence for each coverage declaration. The engine uses exact decimal arithmetic for those declared controls. It does not infer a tariff formula, VAT treatment, rounding rule, invoice, currency conversion, payment instruction or debtor balance.

| Financial question | Source requirement and visible result |
| --- | --- |
| What amount does a row represent? | Original amount field, decimal convention and explicit sign meaning. |
| Can two totals be combined? | Matching declared currency and source partition; no currency conversion is invented. |
| What links an allocation to charge/payment? | Exact original compound endpoint identity and explicit allowed namespace/period relationships. |
| Is a residual qualified? | All required charge, payment and allocation coverage declarations must be complete for that source partition. |
| What remains unknown? | Invalid decimals, absent signs, unresolved endpoints, incomplete coverage and unconfirmed policy remain visible findings. |
