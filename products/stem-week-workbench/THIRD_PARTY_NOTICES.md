# Third-party source

The eight original flower-farm-math files are retained byte-for-byte under vendor/flower-farm-math, with original MIT license. Source repository: https://github.com/SaschaHeyer/flower-farm-math . The browser core includes the exact original reconcile implementation with only its module export keyword removed for classic-script composition. The maintained archive codec supplies UTF-8 and SHA-256 support. No external runtime fetch or credentials are required.
