# Review the merchant's complete financial workflow

Open the [complete merchant financial walkthrough](../../stellar-forge/developer-site/dist/grant-data/financial-walkthrough.md). It is the maintained operating procedure for presenting the useful paid result, original customer invoice, received money, refund or credit, bank reconciliation, customer close and accountant receiving.

The same complete guide is readable at **/grant-financial-walkthrough.html** in the installed SCF application reader. Run **starforge grant-application --port 8787**, then follow **Financial walkthrough**. Its contents, full command blocks, tables, downloads and browser print view use the existing reviewer interface.

The walkthrough starts with either the selected merchant's original work or the completed portable customer billing case. The latter retains the complete 243-file application, original paid and open USD 4,400 milestone invoices, both planning cases and the account that completed independent receiving. The original package and source-owned opener retain later work when that account moves again.

## Select the actual application

Use the original product registrations for the customer task:

| Selection | Installed work |
| --- | --- |
| merchant-receivables | Invoices, customer money, wallet collection/refunds, payment plans and the original receivables MCP and customer-closing clients. |
| merchant-receivables-xero | Complete original invoice/credit document packages, organisation mappings and Xero receiving/cash commissioning. |
| merchant-bank-statements | Original bank statement receiving, exact customer money and fee postings, source corrections and retained account closes. |
| grant-reviewer | Complete submitted application, financial walkthrough, saved submission/review and original bundled purchase workbench. |

The full procedure includes the source builder, runtime installer, Applications and accounting-source setup. Companion applications reuse the customer's original accounting state under its current single writer. The complete [financial operations guide](FINANCIAL_OPERATIONS.md) remains the detailed per-product reference.

## Continue the original demonstration

The merged public financial product is [PR2583](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/public-commons-sprint-2026/pull/2583), with 139 joined paths and 46 original receivables operations. Its 275-file current source capsule preserves the original invoice and MCP programmes for the existing receiving operation. The completed portable customer account, original bank example and their selected source editions have their own retained operating results.

The application connects this shipped foundation to its existing USD 38,000 / 400-hour / 150-day future delivery programme. Its original purchase register and customer-adoption measures retain their selected source and meaning.
