# Harvest, forecast and outcome source contract

The complete engine follows physical produce, predicted quality and actual commercial dispositions through sixteen canonical source families. The original framework supplies exact document bytes and historical resolve/document/authorize callbacks. Source values are scalar strings or null. An explicit blank remains a known blank; null or missing mapped cells remain unobserved and cannot silently become zero or an open interval.

All source families use immutable exact version IDs, business record keys, consecutive revisions, original source documents and both recording and availability instants. A later faithful export can preserve a row's original availability. The selected export must exist at assessment; each original row and callback must exist at the historical decision it supports.

External resolve kinds: grower, site (grower_id), crop (unit), location (site_id), logger (location_id, valid_from, valid_to), person, buyer, protocol, invoice_line (buyer_id,currency,gross_amount,dispatch_record_id,source_document_id). Each returns its exact id and original available_at. document returns id/available_at; authorize(role,personId,scopeId,at) returns authorized/available_at/source_document_id, for warehouse, quality-assessor, route-planner and finance. No credential or authority is inferred from a business website.

Harvested batches are roots. Derived batches preserve their root family through quantity-labelled transfers; splits and merges require the same crop and unit. A derived batch's whole created quantity must be explained by retained incoming transfers. Its earliest harvest time must match its roots. Transfers, actual dispatch and warehouse dispositions consume physical stock. Routing plans are proposed allocations and do not consume stock. A wrong or unapproved dispatch remains an actual physical movement with a separately unresolved plan binding.

Storage records explicitly bind an entire batch and location over an interval. Split portions use distinct derived batches. Logger rows are recorded interval summaries with exact min/max/mean Celsius, not interpolated point samples. Their scope and full interval must match storage and logger identity. Overlapping readings are unresolved; gaps and only partially observed storage remain visible.

Forecasts import actual model outputs. The evaluator does not invent a cultivar decay law. Model artifact and training manifest are original documents; training root record keys make all descendants of the same harvest one family for holdout isolation. Exact input manifests bind source rows known at forecast origin. Generation time may be later for a historical replay, while model release, training cutoff and every underlying feature remain constrained by the original prediction time. All split siblings share the root exclusion.

Quality endpoints require actual observations under the same supplied protocol. A best-before label, dispatched shipment, accepted parcel or absence of complaint is not a shelf-life endpoint. Whole-batch representative usable/unusable assessments define interval, left or right censoring; partial observations remain visible. Interval-distance is an absolute-error lower bound and is labelled that way. Unobserved outcomes stay in the forecast population.

Demand records retain actual buyer quantity, delivery window and required usable hours after arrival. Proposed plan lines compare forecast hours with the recorded requirement and retain uncertainty. Operator reviews bind the exact whole plan and all lines. Actual dispatch, terminal disposition quantities, observed sales and posted credits are distinct source families. Amounts retain exact rational arithmetic and original currency; no routing estimate becomes revenue or loss avoided.

Each terminal outcome is one non-overlapping portion of an actual dispatch. Later corrections revise that outcome. A sold portion and its subsequent return use a retained successor disposition and separate posted credit, rather than counting the same portion twice. Outstanding quantities remain visible.

Physical identity persists across the same batch record_key revision chain. Every movement keeps its exact original version reference, while current inventory and derived-lot ancestry reconcile that stable physical lot. A voided current batch source with retained movements yields unresolved inventory. All simultaneous source-lot consumptions must fit the parent quantity at the actual split. Complete plan approval also requires the declared source family counts. A posted credit's invoice line must bind the same physical dispatch as its referenced outcome, with the original invoice document available then.
