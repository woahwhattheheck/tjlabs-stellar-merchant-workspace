### Service-directory commissioning and referral destinations

A directory maintainer needs to turn a changing list of organisations, services, locations, eligibility rules and opening times into a usable referral destination. The implementation payer commissions that integration; the directory publisher owns the source and its reuse terms; the referral team reviews the appropriate service; and the receiving engineer operates the installed application. The delivered Human Services Directory product connects those roles to the existing Gravity referral workflow.

**The operating journey.** Staff import the original HSDS JSON, CSV tables or archive, FHIR resources or retained provider capture. Each source keeps its publisher, original file, snapshot or incremental scope, complete records and relationships. Staff freeze an edition, inspect changed and unresolved identities against its predecessor, and review the full source-to-FHIR mapping. The saved service choice carries the original candidate list, eligibility, capacity and schedule interpretation. It distinguishes operating hours, events and exclusions and preserves supplied zero and null values. An explicit join binds that selected service to the original Gravity Task and ServiceRequest. The receiving engineer opens the complete editable directory and referral package, including earlier editions and the decisions used to select the destination. The [operating guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/10e017744344ce831bd78fc1ea4c2e3143172d29/products/human-services-directory/OPERATING.md) gives the native, browser, connected and installed workflow.
