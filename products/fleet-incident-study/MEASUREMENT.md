# Read the fleet account

## Useful work and cost

Define one actual application and useful-output unit before combining assignments. The authored course defines an accepted move of a specified load over a declared warehouse route. Other applications need their own source definition and context review.

The complete rate is:

**sum of eligible recognised cost / sum of eligible accepted useful output**

The desk retains failed zero-output assignments in that denominator population. It does not average per-assignment rates or use engine hours as production. Missing output coverage, unresolved selection or missing cost coverage suppresses the complete rate. A fully covered rejected-output interval contributes zero useful output. Conversion requires a positive exact numerator and denominator plus its source.

Exact rational arithmetic preserves original decimal strings. Display decimals are presentations of retained numerator/denominator values. Different currencies, tax bases and economic bases remain separate. Currency precision is declared in the plan; enter the actual convention rather than assuming two places for every currency.

## Time layers

Calendar time out on rent is distinct from operating, idle and scheduled unavailable time. The rental layer may overlap operational observations. Different operational states cannot simultaneously classify the same asset/assignment interval; conflicts remain visible and affected complete totals are null.

Same-state intervals are unioned. Asset downtime is unioned by physical asset and planned/unplanned category; job interruption is unioned separately by job. A replacement can restore the job while the first asset stays down. Different assets' simultaneous downtime remains separate asset-time. Open continuously observed downtime is censored at the declared boundary, with that status retained.

Meter deltas use actual endpoint readings and units. An explicit rollover can explain a negative apparent change; an unknown reset cannot. A meter pair spanning outside the chosen window is not silently prorated. Sparse telemetry remains an observation unless its source supplies a valid aggregation interval and coverage.

## Financial recognition and settlement

Incurred or posted expenses are recognised according to the declared source basis. Estimates and proposed amounts remain separate. Credits and reversals preserve original references, signs and actual chronology. A hypothetical rental rate multiplied by downtime is not an actual source expense or an issued customer credit.

Allocate an original supplier line once across the actual jobs or assets. All allocations must reconcile to the retained whole-line total before a complete projection is shown. An independent line with the same item description remains independent.

Cash is a separate settlement account. All rows sharing a processor and external transaction identity reconcile globally before any job or asset view. Exact duplicate observations do not create another payment; conflicting allocations make all affected cash projections unresolved. Cash must reference the correct reconciled cost and matching asset, assignment, currency and economic basis. Refunds share the original payment's lineage and amount limit.

Known subtotals remain inspectable when a complete total is unresolved. Null is not a zero receipt, zero expense or zero cost per unit.

## Historical knowledge and human findings

A review question separates its decision instant from its knowledge cutoff. Retained historical extract selections determine what source was available. Later repair labels can explain a current case without becoming predictors in a frozen pre-event report.

A report records exact source rows and parents. Their source availability, approvals and clocks are evaluated against the historical question, including the containing extract. A later source edition can differ visibly from a frozen input without rewriting that input.

These observations support reconstruction and discussion. A simple retrospective comparison does not identify a causal effect or reproduce a supplier's proprietary risk model.

## Whole work burden

Record all recurring phases: collection, identity/time reconciliation, mapping, engineering review, correction, finance reconciliation and feedback, plus any actual added phases. Keep initial setup separately. Count active person-minutes from actual observations; elapsed downtime is not staff effort.

Only the same matched group and complete phase coverage produce a complete paired change. Negative change means less observed work; positive change means more. Empty or incomplete effort sources remain unresolved. Duplicate work identifiers and overlapping observations for the same person cannot increase a complete total. The report retains unmatched and setup rows for inspection.

The authored course's 70→56 person-minutes includes every declared recurring phase. It is a worked exercise, not a claim about an actual fleet or a cash saving.
