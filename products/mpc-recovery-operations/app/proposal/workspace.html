Model method

Units: TB and Gbps are decimal (10¹² bytes and 10⁹ bits/second). The issued 4 Gbps is a planning throughput figure; the additional realised-throughput factor is an explicit scenario adjustment. No hardware performance, compression or supplier price is supplied by this workbook.

Server capacity: projected protected volume = baseline + annual growth × selected year offset. Stored full size = projected volume × stored/raw factor. Each retained complete chain contains one full and the selected number of restore points at the entered backup-start interval. Changed bytes per point = projected volume × daily change fraction × interval/24, capped at the whole dataset. Incremental chains retain (points−1) × changed bytes per point. Differential chains retain the sum of point-number × changed bytes since the chain full, capped at the whole dataset for each point. Changed bytes are a user assumption; repeated changes and platform block reuse can make a different physical result.

Peak repository capacity: retained fulls + retained changed data + additional independent archive fulls + temporary full-copy equivalents, all divided by (1−required free fraction). Archives are additional physical full equivalents; do not enter a shared/overlapping synthetic reference as another independent copy. The defaults illustrate four independent seven-point chains at 24-hour intervals. They are a modelling example, and the prime selects the future retention policy and real physical storage factors.

Transfer: hours = transferred TB × 8,000 ÷ (effective Gbps × 3,600). Initial seeding always includes a complete server dataset. Active periodic fulls repeat that wire volume. A synthetic periodic full has zero full WAN retransmission in this model, while daily changes still transfer; local I/O, server load, metadata, synthesis and platform-specific recoverability require separate design and measurement.

Recovery-point age: the displayed scenario assumes serial jobs, a usable recovery point captured at job start and no intermediate usable checkpoint. The conservative age bound is max(start interval, longest modelled transfer duration) + longest modelled transfer duration. The nominated fixed cadence is flagged infeasible when a transfer exceeds its interval. This covers transfer time only; processing and verification increase it. The nominal oldest-to-newest retained-point span is (number of points−1) × start interval; it is not a guarantee of actual retained age. Scheduling, overlapping jobs and the actual platform's checkpoint semantics must be resolved in the selected architecture. An RPO is measured from the latest usable protected point, not from the latest green job status.

Microsoft 365 / Entra: volume and SaaS product entitlements are separate from the server-chain calculation. A blank volume remains unknown. Price the actual mailbox/site/non-user/identity scope and retention through the chosen product; do not assume server licences cover it.

Quotation: blank prices stay unquoted. Every complete quote row needs a quantity, price and supplier/source entry. A blank cloud quantity follows the current server-repository capacity; override it with the actual quoted billable quantity. Four-year figures use those fixed quantities and each row's explicit escalation factor, with one-time costs only in year 1. They are partial known subtotals until all rows are sourced. Annual renewal remains conditional on MPC approval and agreement.
