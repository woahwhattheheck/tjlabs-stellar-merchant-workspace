# Restaurant Integration Evaluation Desk

## Purpose

Use this independent workbench to evaluate an actual restaurant integration with supplied product records and retained Compass API journals. It preserves the full case inventory, exact request and response bytes, explicit restaurant selection, profile-bound decisions, displayed wording, corpus reconciliation and recurring operator effort. It does not generate a dietary score or certify a restaurant.

The ordinary desktop starts empty. The complete authored workflow is explicitly labelled: five fictional cases exercise one fit, one actual unknown verdict, an ambiguous Enrich result, a dispatched timeout and an out-of-coverage request. Its fixture responses are authored examples, not calls made to the provider. Actual connected runs use the separate native request runner and an owner-supplied environment key.

## Source intake and revisions

Twenty-one canonical tables are defined by schema.cjs and templates/*.csv. Import an original UTF-8 CSV, choose its field mapping and retain the entire original. Unmapped means unobserved and remains null; a mapped empty cell remains an observed blank. The selected extract has its own source availability instant. Import/capture time and historical availability are separate facts.

Every canonical record retains an immutable id, stable record_key, positive revision, explicit supersedes_id, active/void status, recorded_at, available_at and source reference. A later edition does not erase the previous original. Competing unsuperseded revisions remain unresolved. Historical selection requires the actual retained source edition rather than silently substituting a newer file.

Supporting documents preserve exact bytes, filename, type, SHA-256 and source availability. A changed availability annotation is a guarded correction to its exact previous metadata. Original/returned comparisons retain record-field differences and typed attachment-byte differences separately from integration success.

## Tables

Products identify the actual user surface and nonsecret account alias. People record who observed, selected, approved or reviewed work and the actual period of their authority. Places retain the product's own identity and caller-held external identifiers. Profiles retain the exact diet, cross-contamination preference and dietary rules supplied in the request.

Cases define the complete consecutive population. Use included, excluded with a reason, or unknown. An ambiguous identity, failed dispatch or out-of-coverage response remains an included case when it belongs to the declared population. The expected case count and coverage declaration must reconcile before a whole-population fraction is shown.

Requests bind an entire verified operation journal document. Attempts provide one canonical observation for every actual dispatch in that journal. Missing, duplicate or altered attempt observations are surfaced. They cannot turn three retries into one billable call or hide an unanswered dispatch.

Selections record a named person's choice of one exact retained Search result. Mappings record a confirmed Enrich identity, explicit retirement or a later mapping replacement. Adoptions bind the exact Decide response, restaurant identity, approved profile, actual displayed wording, risk list, verification requirement, reviewer and time. Evaluations record a distinct observed criterion with pass, fail or unknown; provider confidence remains provider output and is not substituted for the evaluation.

Corpus snapshots and members retain a product's own complete saved-place inventory. Change pages retain a fixed query and actual page responses. Usage observations distinguish cumulative monthly snapshots from source-backed incremental ledger transactions. Price rules retain any explicitly approved later pricing source without filling an unlisted price. Effort records all recurring phases and exact work identities. Reports and report inputs freeze named source rows, exact extracts, hashes and knowledge cutoffs. Report reviews record named role decisions bound to the exact report and complete input list. Input bindings, historical case coverage and role acceptance are reported separately. A report needs its explicit population window, expected count, complete case-row inventory and all named roles before overall completeness. Source documents provide an optional mapped register of retained originals.

## Identity and dietary decisions

Search is discovery. Its match_for_profile field is a preview, not a final fit verdict. A Search result must be selected explicitly; the workbench never chooses candidate zero. Enrich must return matched:true and a nonblank exact compass_id before it can establish the common integration identity path. An HTTP 200 unmatched or ambiguous response remains unresolved.

The native prepare-decision command binds the upstream operation hash, exact response and body hashes, selected Compass ID, selector and selection time. The call engine checks the complete upstream journal again before dispatch. A malformed timestamp or altered binding is refused. The study also checks the actual selected case profile at dispatch. Returned identity must equal the requested identity before a verdict is adopted.

The documented native path uses a persisted Compass ID even though the dedicated Decide endpoint also documents a caller-held Google ID. This avoids the conflicting cached integration examples and preserves a common supported Enrich→Compass ID→Decide path.

An actual decision of unknown is retained as a verdict when attached to a resolved identity. It remains distinct from an unresolved response that has no decision. A fit result does not remove risks, verification_required, freshness or provider qualifications. Verbatim adoption must match the exact recommended_user_text. A reviewed adaptation records the actual named reviewer and preserves the full provider response; the workbench does not judge whether the rewritten prose is medically safe.

## Durable actual calls

The network runner is separate from the offline operator. Credentials are read only from COMPASS_API_KEY in the native owner environment. Request files, source bundles and journals contain no credentials. Only the official HTTPS API origin and five current routes are supported. Redirects are refused.

Before network dispatch, the exact operation and dispatch intent are durably journalled. All response headers and bytes, partial reads, transport uncertainty and retry decisions remain retained. The same exact operation ID returns the existing journal and cannot silently replay a dispatched call. A different request under the same ID is rejected.

Only documented transient per-minute/IP 429 responses receive bounded automatic retry. Monthly quota, entitlement, validation and lost-response uncertainty stop. The actual native wrapper documents its Linux Node 20+ and util-linux flock dependency. The portable engine and offline study do not require that runtime.

The operator imports either one complete compass.journal/v1 receipt or a whole compass.native-export/v1 envelope. Complete export originals and every listed immutable/partial filesystem artifact are retained before derived rows are selected. A failure validates no partial successor into the active study.

## Corpus and metadata

Compass IDs are actual current record identities; they are not a promise that a place identifier will survive every rebuild. Preserve the product's own place key separately. Two complete snapshots may show added, removed and remapped product places. A removal from a product snapshot is an observed inventory change, not proof that a restaurant closed.

A complete Changes run requires every page from the same since/type/page-size/scope query and reconciled totals. It still is not a complete city inventory: redirects, merges and rebuilds may require a separate full reconciliation. Preserve the complete native responses rather than treating one page as exhaustive.

Metadata fields remain verbatim, including top-level scoringVersion and model_versions.scoring when their values differ. Counts in documentation are examples. A 304 response is a revalidation and contains no replacement metadata payload.

## Costs and effort

The published 15 October 2026 credit model is fast Search 1, rich Search 2, fast Enrich 1, fast Decide 2 and rich Decide 5. Rich Enrich and Changes prices are unlisted in the retained public pricing contract and remain unknown. Metadata is unmetered. A custom rule needs its own actual source, validity period, approval and exact decimal amount.

Modelled per-dispatch credits are an explicit estimate, including retry dispatches. Charging for failed or unanswered calls is not inferred. Monthly usage headers are cumulative snapshots: never add them together or assign their differences to one caller amid concurrent activity. Only unique source-backed provider ledger transactions are summed as observed incremental credits; they remain a subset unless separately reconciled to the period.

Record active person-minutes for intake, identity selection, integration, review, corrections, reconciliation and feedback, or a declared replacement phase set. Work keys identify the actual work independently of filenames. Duplicate work and overlapping active work for one person remain unresolved. All included cases and both methods need complete phase coverage for a whole-population effort change. Empty input is unobserved, not zero. The signed current-minus-baseline difference can be negative or positive. It is not a causal effect or a cash saving.

## Review, ordinary recovery and exports

Reviews bind the complete current study digest. Editing a selected input or plan leaves earlier review history intact and requires a current review. Ordinary unfinished forms, prior studies, imported candidates and competing browser branches remain in complete portable session recovery. The source-bank design deduplicates immutable records without dropping prior versions.

Exports include the whole study/session, current canonical CSVs, original CSVs, column availability, exact binary documents, readable report, complete analysis and maintained application source. CSVs are a readable projection; reopen the whole session to retain draft forms and branches. Native corrections must bind both study ID and selected extract ID before a row index is applied.
