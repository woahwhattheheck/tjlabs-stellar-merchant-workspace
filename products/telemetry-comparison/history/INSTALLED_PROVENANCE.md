# Installed application source

The complete 58-file generic telemetry source is retained from Commons PR37929, commit 5f3850948a79dd7511dba3fee276f3338c91e6a9, tools/telemetry_comparison_workspace. Its original builder, controller and manifest are retained under history/before-installed.

Managed private storage, exact request recovery, Applications registration and full browser-branch continuation are composed from the maintained import-host-reconciliation product, Merchant PR2090, commit f7cc63392f9a9b89791d43ec3e8d4a8b660be92f. The model dispatch and source/read/report projections are adapted to the original telemetry API; the complete telemetry numerical engine remains unchanged.

All donor history and MIT notices remain. Customer, commercial and private connected input are outside this source package.
