# Equipment Service and Entitlement Study Desk — operating contract

This companion reconstructs supplied distributor service records. It keeps an original customer unit, its parent system or component, a service case, an approved procedure, commercial work and an issued document separate. It evaluates the consistency and completeness of those records. A complete record chain is not an equipment serviceability decision, a prescribed test result, or permission to release equipment.

## Complete source and historical meaning

Each canonical row retains its immutable version ID, stable record key, revision, explicit predecessor, active or void status, recording time, office-availability time and original source locator. Corrections add a retained edition. They do not erase the preceding record or original bytes. The original CSV, mapping, line location, mapped blank and unmapped column remain distinguishable. Binary originals retain full bytes, media type, name and digest.

A historical view uses only source editions and record versions available at that instant. Effective dates remain separate from recording and office receipt. Future-effective context must not erase the context that applied earlier. Historical reports bind exact source edition, row digest and original document. A later certificate correction or newly received photograph may improve a later report; it cannot retroactively support an earlier delivery.

## Customer, site, department and unit

Organisations identify actual customers and service organisations. Sites can form an explicit customer-owned hierarchy, with departments retained by name and site identity. Assets retain customer, site, parent asset, manufacturer, model, serial, tag, ownership and actual configuration. A fixed system and a replaceable sensor remain separate assets. A rental substitute keeps its own identity; receiving it does not rename the customer's original unit.

Identity resolution requires retained source context. Similar model names and repeated tags are not a reason to merge units. Missing identifiers, conflicting serial observations, cycles in parent relationships and cross-customer site links remain unresolved. Source-system identity links record the actual person and time of confirmation. No guessed serial, manufacturer entitlement or software identifier is supplied.

## Procedures, forms, eligibility and due obligations

A procedure is an actual approved source edition for a manufacturer, model and action. Its original form remains a retained document. The required-item definition preserves actual item keys, allowed answers, required responses and evidence requirements from that source. Each inspection binds the exact procedure version and canonical digest, plus the asset configuration used.

Model-specific service eligibility is supplied separately. Stocking equipment does not imply authority or capability to service every model. Supported, unsupported and unresolved observations remain distinct. Actual technical authorities record named permissions, scope, validity interval and grant source.

Due obligations carry their own action, due instant, cadence text, basis and actual authorisation. Several obligations may apply to one unit. The application does not invent annual or other standard intervals. A replacement starts a separate asset history. An explicit future obligation is different from an overdue obligation and from a completed service event.

## Intake, inspection, findings and performed work

The complete consecutive case inventory records the received time, customer, site, original asset, requested action, inclusion decision, reason for any exclusion and actual reported status. Pending, declined, failed, unsupported and unresolved cases remain visible. A whole-population fraction requires reconciled identity, time, explicit selection and expected count; an unknown case cannot silently become an exclusion.

An inspection retains its actual start and completion, named inspector, reported result, exact procedure and configuration bindings, completed item observations, evidence and signature original. The companion checks the supplied form's required fields and evidence. It does not create answers, sign for a technician, copy a former inspection's pass result or replace a prescribed form with an inferred generic checklist.

A deficiency retains its unit, case, inspection/item, source description, actual observation and dated state. Repair, replacement, approved no-action, awaiting approval and declined work have different meanings. Actual performed work retains the responsible person, action, dates, quantity, unit, disposition and original result. A failed repair remains performed work and does not become a successful repair.

Where the supplied procedure requires a reinspection or closure evidence, the actual records must provide it. A customer commercial approval alone is not technical closure. Conversely, a technician's completed work does not establish that the customer authorised its price or quantity.

## Commercial observations

The separate service-commerce module supplies 15 explicitly namespaced families. They retain actual scoped authority, exact quote-line editions, partial approvals, work allocation, purchased-unit first-service entitlements, replacement-linked charges and credits, warranty decisions and settlements, rental terms and events, invoice lines and payments.

Quotation, approval, work consumed, invoiced amount, posted credit, observed cash and rental accrual remain separate observations. A benefit binds the actual purchased unit and policy edition; a first service already consumed cannot be presented as available. A proposed replacement-fee waiver is separate from an actual posted credit. Manufacturer warranty eligibility, a submitted claim, a decision and a settlement remain separate.

Currencies, tax basis, quantity and units come from the source. There is no automatic exchange rate or assumed tax rate. Financial transaction identity must be reconciled globally before projection into a case or invoice. Missing or contradictory source context stays unresolved.

Physical custody has its own records. A rental charge does not prove that a substitute was issued or returned. Custody retains the actual unit, parties, actor, effective time and source reference.

## Certificates, customer delivery and reports

A certificate retains exact case and unit identity, procedure version, relevant inspection and actual performed-work references, purpose, supplied disposition, original document, issue/signature times and named signatory. A later replacement certificate explicitly identifies its predecessor. Neither a filename nor a customer report title proves the attachment belongs to the intended unit.

Publication, delivery, acknowledgement and withdrawal are actual dated events. Their exact document and case/unit bindings remain visible. The original incorrect send remains in history when a later correct send occurs. At each delivery instant, evaluate the certificate and evidence known at that instant. A later withdrawal affects later currentness without rewriting an earlier recorded delivery.

Frozen reports specify their own historical population, expected count, source scope and required review roles. Every source input binds a retained edition, immutable row and canonical digest or original document. Named report review binds the whole report and input list. Input validity, population coverage and review completion remain separate outputs.

## Whole recurring work

Record actual active person-minutes, not elapsed waiting, for intake, identity work, inspection preparation, quotation, approval handling, actual service record handling, customer packet preparation, corrections and follow-up. Use a declared phase set and matched case identity. Duplicate filenames do not define distinct work; a stable work key does. Overlapping active work for one person remains unresolved.

A paired subset may have a valid signed current-minus-baseline difference while other cases have no baseline. Report that subset and all observed current work separately. A whole-population change requires all eligible cases, both methods and every required phase. An empty source is unobserved; an explicit zero is an observation. Work reductions are not automatically realised cash savings.

## Review, recovery and exact exports

Current study reviews bind the complete selected source and plan digest. Earlier acceptance remains historical after changes. Full portable sessions retain unfinished forms, import candidates, prior studies, compacted immutable history and competing browser branches. A failed save must preserve the exact pending form for recovery.

Ordinary exports contain readable reports, canonical CSV projections, column availability, full original files, documents, source editions, review history, complete session and maintained application source. Native record corrections bind study ID and selected extract ID before applying a row index. Use the complete session to move devices without losing pending work.

## Historical technical bindings

An intake binds the customer unit and site at actual receipt. An inspection or work observation uses its actual event time; a later legitimate unit movement does not rewrite the earlier service. Certificate support is evaluated using inspection and original evidence available at issuance, work available at signing, and actual deficiencies known at issuance. Later office arrivals cannot retrospectively support the earlier certificate. A required repair reinspection must start after the actual completed repair, while the original finding and all preceding states remain retained.

Distinct current source records claiming the same business report ID are unresolved. They cannot share an input list and appear as two complete reports. Report-source bindings, full population and named review completion remain separately visible.
