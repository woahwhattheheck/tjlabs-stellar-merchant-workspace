4. **Receive and continue the whole working project.** Export the original source, reviews, publisher generations, prepared buyer configurations, acquired sessions and delivered files. A second engineer supplies destination runtime references, restores the original publisher and acquisition, delivers saved output and selects a later publisher generation. Export that progressed project for another receiver, then reopen the same original incoming handover at the first destination.
