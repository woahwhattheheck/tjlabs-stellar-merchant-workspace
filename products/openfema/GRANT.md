## Public Assistance portfolio commissioning and recurring review

Recovery programme analysts can commission a complete review of an agreed disaster cohort: retain original declarations, applicants and obligated projects; resolve source joins; assign follow-ups; and deliver a reviewed edition into the programme’s existing system. The workorder names the payer, merchant, independent buyer, receiving engineer, useful output and recurring purchase.

The [merged OpenFEMA exchange](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/public-commons-sprint-2026/blob/a5eaac406a60502246e45cf003761340bcf355e2/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf30-seller-sdk/openfema/DATA_INTEGRATION.md) receives the destination schema, retains its reviewed field mapping, exports every selected project, reconciles the complete customer CSV and imports selected analyst decisions into a new native version. Its browser, CLI, 41-tool merchant and typed client share the original workspace. The DR 4724 example contains **195 funded projects, 43 applicants and two declaration areas**. The full native run matched all 195 unchanged rows, applied one internal analyst decision with all four financial totals preserved, and reopened and restored the complete archive.

The October 12 DR 4935 source supplies an early-programme edition: **23 declaration areas, zero applicant rows and zero funded-project rows**. The exchange retains that starting edition and customer-only records awaiting source publication. The merchant prepares the accepted version and payment terms; the buyer receives the complete agreed output, source, mapping and review history.

For a selected installation, **T1** delivers the destination mapping, host, buyer integration and independent handover. **T2** deploys the useful operation through both selected facilitators and recovers interrupted payment or delivery. **T3** completes recurring mainnet use, release/rollback and receiving-host recovery. These customer-specific adaptations remain within the existing **$38,000 / 400-hour / 150-day**, two-merchant/two-buyer plan and its original usage targets.
