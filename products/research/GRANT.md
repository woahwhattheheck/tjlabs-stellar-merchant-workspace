## Collaborative Research delivery and reviewed dossier returns

A research team and its customer need to continue the same evidence dossier after both have made edits. The [dossier-return operating workflow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/89bcf7629b0133df094c34df871c0124697adb6b/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/dossier-returns/OPERATING.md) retains the complete returned file, identifies the shared saved ancestor and compares it with the team's current dossier. The reviewer sees each differing item's full original, current and returned values, chooses an allowed treatment and saves a named decision. The [original return engine](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/89bcf7629b0133df094c34df871c0124697adb6b/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/dossier-returns/engine.mjs) validates the complete result against its source and current revision before committing it. Earlier work, the returned branch and the accepted revision remain available together.

The shipped browser, authenticated client, installed command and Merchant tools use those same saved comparisons, reviewer drafts and requests. The [customer handover desk](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/52ca056d8991ed68ecb721c885e4c9096dbd125b/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/customer-updates/HANDOVER_DESK.md) brings the full operation into a private local interface: upload the original return, inspect complete values, save explicit named decisions, recover unfinished edits and download the accepted reports and editable package. The [handover programme](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4541dba01faa0e8121866fbb273be65a74542af0/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/customer-updates/handover-returns.mjs) enforces the selected customer's cumulative return and item limits while retaining earlier accepted rounds. A lost response reopens the original request. Specialized eligibility, extraction, appraisal and citation proposals retain their original sources for the corresponding review. The receiving organization opens the accepted dossier and continues editing its original history.

The [complete customer programme](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/0287e051ef121e9842072f93ad839378cdc06af4/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/customer-programme/README.md) supplies the preceding delivery: selected original documents, citation review, chronology, Europe PMC source operations, Python analysis, an editable workbook and a complete source-bearing package. The merged return product adds the subsequent customer review operation. Its [full native receiving programme](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/6af7dfe3845f339cdd7e03619c348fc94dff6e29/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/dossier-returns/source-native-acceptance.mjs) carries retained documents through branch review, browser operation, portable handover and independent editable reopening. The current original receiving operation has completed its document conversion and has its full source dependencies supplied. The separate [handover desk receiving course](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/52ca056d8991ed68ecb721c885e4c9096dbd125b/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/customer-updates/handover-desk-receiving.mjs) operates actual selected return files and reviewed decisions through the browser, request recovery, complete package and independent reopening. Source review is complete; these original equipped-worker continuations provide the native execution result.

For commissioning, the workshare identifies the recurring research question, permitted sources, named reviewer, customer receiving installation, return date and useful accepted output. A research agency can deliver an evidence update, receive the customer's corrections, review the changed dossier and hand over the accepted edition with its complete source and decision history. T1 connects that selected customer's source, host and operating procedure to the implemented workflow. The existing T2 and T3 milestones then measure independent receiving, repeat purchase and production handover of the agreed service.
