# Fleet Availability Study Desk

A complete study of actual depot membership, readiness, maintenance, parts and supplier dependencies, safety release, duty execution and recurring staff work across 29 source families. The application retains source and returned file editions and supporting original bytes. Historical decisions use the records available at their actual time. A revision keeps the same business identity; later descriptive corrections preserve earlier fault, work and duty history.

Readiness requires an observed state and its actual release basis. Repair completion and parts arrival alone do not release a vehicle. Release inputs retain exact completion rows and hashes; named safety and operations reviews bind the complete requirement population. The application records source decisions; actual operating authority remains with the named operator.

Elapsed time uses unioned in-fleet intervals for each physical vehicle. Overlapping workshop, parts and safety waits describe the same vehicle-time. Unknown time stays visible. Actual duty departures, cancellations, substitutions, completions and returns remain visible; fulfilment additionally requires compatible physical capacity, readiness, completion within the approved service window, and no overlapping actual use.

Fault history retains open and unresolved episodes alongside completed recovery durations. Actual invoice, credit and cost postings retain original amounts and currencies. Duplicate observations of the same external transaction are reconciled once; conflicting transaction observations leave that total unresolved. Eight recurring-work phases compare complete matched members and retain all current effort, including incomplete comparisons.
