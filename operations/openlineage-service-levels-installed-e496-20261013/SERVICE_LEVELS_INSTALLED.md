This programme opens the original OpenLineage Service Levels customer through the maintained selected product installation. It keeps the original workspace, policy versions, assessments, source events and resolved incident, and delivers a complete editable workspace with the current application source.

The programme lives beside this guide in operations/openlineage-service-levels-installed-e496-20261013. Its operation is OPENLINEAGE-SERVICE-LEVELS-INSTALLED-E496-20261013. The earlier operating case is SCF-OPENLINEAGE-SERVICE-LEVELS-BBBD-20261012, workspace 74c371a0-9689-41d4-bcf2-3f0f57f5bebe, revision 13.

The installation contains the complete selected product archive and its recorded source commit, the original 66-file version 4 application and original export, and a newly received workspace carrying the selected current application. Both editions retain the same thirteen original operation identities and complete original object bytes.

The operator journey covers:

- The complete original six-tool Service Levels merchant catalogue and original policy request replay.
- An actual application stop and restart, explicit merchant instance reconnection, full workspace export, independent receiving and a second desktop/phone review from the received application source.
- An identical complete re-export after cold reopening and exact original-operation replay.

The programme operates the original Service Levels case. The separately maintained Quality receiving programme and its customer cases keep their own source, workspaces and operation journals.

The earlier parent-only source selection produces a completion-unobserved finding. The operating team assigns and acknowledges it.
