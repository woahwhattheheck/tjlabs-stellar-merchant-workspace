# Technician Day Workbench — complete browser operation

This workbench compares the detailer's declared original records for one owned operating day. It keeps the original and latest booking revisions, explicit technician and bay windows, declared travel/access buffers, independent completion observations and original monetary records together. It creates a comparison and attributed review; the booking system, field observations, invoices and payment processor remain their own authorities.

## Prepare all eight original tables

The complete tables are resources, availability, booking_revisions, quoted_lines, completions, invoice_lines, payments and allocations. The exact columns are shown when a table is selected and retained in INTAKE_CONTRACT.json. Supply all eight even if a source is explicitly header-only.

Each accepted edition has its own identity, label, received-at timestamp, source notes and three exact settings: operating day start, operating day end and as-of instant. Enter complete ISO timestamps with seconds and UTC offsets. The day may span a daylight-saving change or midnight, up to 36 hours; the workbench does not infer a time zone or recurring hours. As-of selects which recorded revisions and observations are current. Future records remain visible separately.

Choose the intended table before importing a file. The receiving operation freezes the selected table and original file before it awaits bytes. UTF-8 BOM and CRLF survive. Original byte receipts remain downloadable independently of the editable source text. If a file is malformed UTF-8, its complete bytes and error remain while the previous draft text stays selected.

Editing a CSV or time setting prepares an unfinished draft. Invalid partial text remains in the full working copy. Receive all eight as a new edition validates the complete set and appends it; earlier accepted editions stay immutable. Copy selected edition into a new draft copies its original source and day settings for an explicit correction. Selecting another retained edition does not replace an unfinished draft.

## Booking identity and resource time

A job has an exact stable customer, vehicle and currency. Full replacement revisions have strictly increasing sequence and recorded-at timestamps. The selected revision is the latest one recorded by the as-of instant; original and later source rows remain visible. Changing a vehicle or customer requires a separate correctly identified job rather than silently rebinding the original history.

Each active booking declares one technician and an optional bay. Each resource has independent integer before/after buffer minutes. These are supplied travel, access, setup or turnaround allowances, never computed road travel. Cancelled and postponed jobs reserve no planned occupancy, but retain their financial records.

Available windows may meet exactly to cover a continuous interval. A declared blocked window takes precedence. Planned overlaps and observed overlaps are reported separately. A service outside the day may still have a declared buffer crossing into it; that occupancy remains visible. A full interval extending outside the owned day is explicitly marked, and its out-of-shift portion is checked against the complete resource windows.

Actual completion snapshots remain independent from the booking status. A completed observation supplies actual start and finish, recorded observation time and assigned resources. An in-progress observation has no fabricated finish. Its observed occupancy stops at observed_at; a later as-of time does not prove that work continued.

The resource selector limits displayed availability, occupancy and conflicts. The complete job/financial comparison and review subject selector remain whole-study views. Calculated occupancy uses explicit UTC timestamps; original source strings and their offsets remain in booking rows and full originals.

## Quotes, invoices, deposits and refunds

Every monetary value is a canonical integer in the currency's declared minor unit. The software groups currencies separately and never converts them. It sums original quoted line amounts tied to the exact booking revision, preserving signed discounts or credits. Missing quoted lines are unknown, not an assumed zero or a public “from” price.

Original invoice lines bind an invoice, job and currency. A negative original line is retained as a credit. A quoted-versus-invoiced difference identifies a source question; it does not create a fee or edit the invoice. Public cancellation, no-show, weather or deposit percentages are not executed automatically. Retain the actual written agreement and authorization with the original records.

A received transaction and a refund are separate positive original amounts. A refund names its earlier received transaction in the same currency. All refunds reduce that receipt's available credit. Over-refund and unallocated refund amounts remain explicit.

Allocations are complete current snapshots with unique IDs. Each links one original transaction to a job and optionally a particular invoice. Deposit, settlement and refund purposes stay explicit. A deposit without an invoice is unapplied job credit; it does not automatically pay one or several invoices. Invoice-linked balances use only allocations to that invoice. The booking's total net funds and net position are shown separately.

Refund allocations must reconcile to the original receipt's job allocations. A refund attributed to a different, previously uncredited job is attention even in the same currency. A mismatched refund does not restore available unused credit. Allocation excess and unallocated transaction balances remain visible.

For a permitted deposit transfer, preserve the customer's written authorization and publish a corrected full allocation table in a new source edition. Reassign the actual original receipt allocation; do not invent a second received payment. The prior edition retains the earlier job credit. A cancelled or weather-postponed job keeps any deposit/invoice balance until actual authorized source records change it.

## Review and correction

Choose an actual booking, resource, invoice, transaction or allocation. The selected full row and related attention are shown alongside the current review predecessor. Record a reviewer, reason and actual evidence reference. Investigate and Accept as expected annotate the original result without changing times or amounts.

A later review is an explicit correction linked to the prior review of that exact edition/type/subject. A stale form cannot overwrite a newer review. Drafts remain keyed to their original source edition and subject when selections change. A new source edition keeps its own source basis and does not silently inherit an earlier approval.
