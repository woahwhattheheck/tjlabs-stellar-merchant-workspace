It compares crew/truck time and money independently. The original scheduling, inspection, signing, filing and payment systems remain their own authorities.

## System, job and promise identity

Customer, property, parcel, jurisdiction, physical system and job are separate source IDs. An address label alone does not merge records. A job's system, customer and currency remain stable through its full replacement revisions. The system master and booking property/location must agree. Keep multiple systems on one property distinct.

An arrival promise retains its accepted window, agreement time and written authorization. The booking names that exact promise. A later schedule revision does not silently change the accepted window. Compare planned time and actual observed arrival to the original accepted promise; a note is not a new agreement.

Each active booking declares a crew and optional truck. Independent before/after buffers are original travel, access or setup allowances. Planned and observed resource conflicts are separate. Available windows may meet to cover continuous time; an explicit blocked window wins. A buffer may overlap the owned day even if the service itself starts outside it. Full intervals extending beyond the day or availability remain visible.

An in-progress completion record does not acquire a fabricated finish. Its observed occupancy ends at the observation time. A later as-of instant does not establish continued work. Cancelled or postponed jobs reserve no planned occupancy while their invoices and credit remain available for review.

## Captured field record and office-known history

field_versions retains the exact job, booking revision, predecessor and source version. The physical captured_at and later observed_at are separate. An offline record is not part of the office-known as-of view until its observation arrives.

The field-values JSON is the original structured record, including any declared measurements or units. The workbench does not turn those observations into a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, cross-unit volume total or compliance result. Customer notes and internal notes remain separate columns and are shown explicitly.

Draft, final and voided are the original record's own status. A final field report does not establish that physical work is complete; the independent completion source provides that observation. Every earlier version remains available when a correction arrives.

## Exact template and document instance

A form template has its original form/version identity, jurisdiction, source URL, document digest where supplied and source-declared required roles. An empty role list means that the requirements were not supplied. It does not mean legal completeness.

A form instance names the exact field version and template version and preserves the original artifact digest. Its created time and office-observed time are separate. A later corrected field record does not rewrite an already signed or filed artifact. The comparison makes the older source binding visible so an operator can decide the next authorized action.

The workbench compares supplied artifact identities; it does not generate a signature, recover a missing document from a hash or certify that a signature is legally valid. Keep the original document available in the authorized source system or complete delivery alongside its reference.

## Signature and filing events

Signature observations bind the actual signature ID, exact form instance, role and signer reference. Signed, declined and revoked events retain their original times and later observation times. A later revocation does not delete the original signature event.

Filing attempts bind the exact form instance, submission time, observed time, destination, channel and authorization. Distinct filing events retain delivered, acknowledged, accepted, rejected or withdrawn. A submitted or delivered form is not automatically acknowledged or accepted. Actual receipt references support source-reported acknowledgement, acceptance and rejection.

Use the actual selected office and form's destination. A vendor's broad county coverage, an operator's service area or a nearby municipal address does not establish the appropriate filing route. A customer report and an authority filing are different handovers.

Repeated attempts and conflicting later outcomes remain visible. Reviews annotate those observations without changing them.

## Exact money and independent settlement

Amounts are canonical integer minor units in explicit currencies. Currencies are never converted. Quoted lines belong to the exact booking revision; a public range is not an accepted job quote. Invoice lines and source credits remain independent of work completion, signature or filing acceptance.

Original received and refund transactions are separate positive amounts. Refunds name the earlier receipt in the same currency. Every refund reduces available credit. A refund cannot remain simultaneously available as unused funds.

Allocations link the actual payment to a job and optionally an invoice. An unapplied deposit is job credit; it does not automatically settle multiple invoices. Invoice balances use only allocations to that exact invoice. A refund allocated to a different, previously uncredited job is flagged even in the same currency.

Retain actual written authority for a transfer or correction and place the full changed allocation source in a new edition. Cancelled or postponed jobs keep their recorded balances until authorized source records change.
