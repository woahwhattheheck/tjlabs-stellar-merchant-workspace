# Delivery Slot Workbench — complete browser operation

The workbench compares the eight original source tables for one or more explicit delivery slots. Its two demand columns deliberately retain different questions: what was accepted at the original cut-off, and what an attributed operator later selected for review. Production and picks are observed current snapshots. Viewing a comparison or recording a review creates no order, production or dispatch event.

## Source identity and cut-off rules

A slot declares its actual offset cut-off and fulfilment timestamp. Its boundary is exactly before or at_or_before; product-specific overrides are explicit original values. The workbench does not infer weekend, regional, wholesale or holiday rules from public prose. Agree the specific actual dates with the supplier.

An order line is the stable slot/order/line tuple. Each numbered revision replaces the whole line. Confirmed means its declared quantity; cancelled means zero while its original revisions remain. Buyer and product identity remain stable within the line. To change a product, use a cancelled old line and a distinct new line. Conflicting or timestamp-inverted revisions need original source correction.

Late revisions remain visible. The original cut-off selection does not change when a later row arrives. The review selector covers all actual lines, products and observed picks across all slots; the Displayed slot filter limits the result tables independently.

## Products, units and observed work

A product has its own base unit and whole/decimal quantity kind. Unit/pack factors and quantity steps come from the exact product's declared mapping. Quantities and conversion factors use canonical exact decimals. Different products/base units never combine into a misleading grand quantity.

Capacity unknown is different from zero. A missing capacity remains unassessed; explicit zero exposes positive demand as excess. Production is one current count snapshot per slot/product with partial or complete coverage. Missing production remains unknown. A correction is a new source edition, not an additive duplicate count.

Each accepted line may have one current picking snapshot; picked and dispatched remain separate actual states. A cancelled or late-only line's observed pick remains visible against the zero original baseline. A report cannot establish that products were made or dispatched.

The product table shows the original fulfilment timestamp, effective cut-off/boundary and source reference beside the named production observation and its timestamp. Picking coverage counts positive reviewed-demand lines. A cancelled zero-demand line does not require a fabricated pick. All observed picks, including picks against zero demand, remain in quantities and attention. Required and observed required line counts are separate from total and observed line counts.

Unfinished review fields are retained per edition and subject, including incomplete selection text. Switch subjects or source editions without losing those drafts. Source amounts only change through a new full source edition.
