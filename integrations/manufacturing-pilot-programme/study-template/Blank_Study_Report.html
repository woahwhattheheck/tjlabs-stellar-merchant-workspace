How to read the measurements

  • elapsed: Explicit cycle end minus start on the original recording timebase.
  • spans: Union of intervals of each kind; concurrent manual and machine spans overlap.
  • manual_track: Sum of within-track manual interval unions, distinct from elapsed time.
  • coverage: Union of all labelled intervals; unlabelled span remains visible.
  • effort: Sum of non-overlapping actual active intervals per recorder. Different people's concurrent work contributes separately.
  • aggregation: Only included cycles within the same study, product revision and station form one group. Original excluded cycles remain.
