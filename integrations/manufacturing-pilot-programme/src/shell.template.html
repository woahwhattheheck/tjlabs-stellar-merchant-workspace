Manufacturing pilot programme

Ten independently researched manufacturers, with published business contacts, observed production workflows and editable introductions.

Study scope and responsibility

Select one actual station, product/process revision and original observation set with its owner. Record permission, the selected analysis product/version and the agreed licence and data-handling arrangement before a study.

Keep baseline cycles, changeover, manual work, machine work, waiting and quality outcomes explicit. Compare analysis effort on the same approved footage or observation set. A production change requires a separate agreed same-job comparison. This desk records setup and supplied observations; it does not analyse video or manufacture measured results.

The manufacturer's existing process instructions, quality criteria and release authority remain the source of technical decisions. Current software, capacity and interest are qualification questions.

Prepare a fixed customer edition

Prepare an edition to freeze the current buyer brief and exactly five profiles. Export that same edition in any format. Private working notes and the internal buyer brief stay in the full workspace.

Internal buyer brief

Export and edit the current brief to record an actual changed request or commercial agreement. Give its successor a new brief_id and name the current brief in parent_brief_id. All earlier customer editions retain their original brief and output bodies.
