## Scientific-array customer commissioning and complete receiving

### A complete scientific customer task

A scientific-data customer needs an agreed array population with its original meaning, followed by an output that another analyst can use and another engineer can maintain. The commissioning record names the source edition, variables, dimension order, coordinate arrays, selected time and geographic population, units, missing-value policy and intended decision. An array index identifies a position; its associated coordinate array supplies the actual time, level, location or ensemble label.

The existing Brightband workshare supplies a concrete customer context. Its original application uses ExtremeWeatherBench 1.1.0 at `7ce3232305532d4ffc021b3c2f8dad41cef3c7e2`: the complete issued California June 7–15, 2022 case 3, FOUR_v200_GFS and PANG_v100_GFS against ERA5, with the original MAE, peak-MAE and RMSE. The original scientific adapter retains the benchmark's alignment, targets, boundaries and scoring. The proposed five-phase engagement is **USD 7,200 over ten business days**, with USD 3,600 at kickoff and USD 3,600 at accepted handover, two consolidated reviews, a sixty-minute handover and ten business days of delivered-scope correction. The original company case and sender continue that proposal. [Original scientific agreement](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/commons/blob/5eceeaacc2b65d7eef4331cf1c2508a2a15e15b9/opportunities/brightband_ewb_receiving_2026/SCIENCE-AND-ACCEPTANCE.md) · [Complete product and workshare](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/commons/tree/c07cb1c1966bee6c36f604c4a8dee9d50e1568c3/opportunities/brightband_ewb_receiving_2026).

The complementary array workflow publishes a reviewed output of the selected customer task and carries its original source and complete receiving state into the customer's application. Source-owner approval determines the actual store and permitted output. Brightband's separately published SUBS product also illustrates why these decisions matter: its forecasts use Zarr, ensemble and lead-time dimensions, explicit initialization and valid-time coordinates, daily-mean values and documented padding. Those public specifications inform the selection worksheet; the existing EWB workshare retains its own issued models, dates and evaluation. ### From source to reviewed delivery

| Step | Complete operating result |
| --- | --- |
| Select source and meaning | Retain the authorized original store/export, edition and rights; inspect hierarchy, native shape, type, attributes, units and coordinate arrays. Record the whole customer population and its expected output. |
| Execute the saved selection | Give every source axis one explicit index or positive-step slice. Run the original native operation and retain all selected cells, source indices, exact types and the original request identity. |
| Review and project | Review the exact result. Choose a String, Number or Boolean representation deliberately; preserve large integers, non-finite values, negative zero and compound types through the supported representation. Keep the native result intact. |
| Publish the useful resource | Attach the accepted frozen result to the original source-dataset publisher. Retain its plan, extraction, target edition, review, published terms and customer receiving configuration. |
| Use and continue | Receive the complete advertised result and its agreed coordinate/source context. Associate the actual purchase with the customer's complete task. A new source edition or genuinely distinct approved query supplies later useful work. |
| Hand over the installation | Receive the original source workspace together with the publisher, dataset, deployment and operator state. A second engineer reopens it, makes the agreed source/terms change, and continues the original operation or retained result. |

A Number projection accepts finite supported numeric values and safe integers; other native representations remain available through an explicitly reviewed text projection. Published rows carry the original result identity, ordinal, source-index vector and selected scalar. Scientific coordinate labels are supplied by the retained coordinate arrays and the agreed customer output. Scientific scores continue to come from the original evaluator.
