## CKAN merchant editions, analytical customer use and staff Support

A CKAN operator or implementation partner can turn a selected catalog resource into a reviewed merchant edition, deliver a usable analytical workspace to its customer, and operate the resulting service through the existing Support desk. The retained source, publication, customer files and staff case stay connected throughout that work. The [complete installed operating guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/9763693a2e0ba2cebf3ee9145be304ec1a0a552d/products/ckan/OPERATING.md) and [whole-workspace receiving guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/9763693a2e0ba2cebf3ee9145be304ec1a0a552d/products/ckan/RECEIVING.md) describe this original product.

### Operator and customer work

The merchant selects its catalog, package, resource and complete query scope. The original CKAN capture retains the actual catalog and resource metadata, every selected DataStore page and the optional original CSVW dictionary. It preserves the original values and numeric precision. A later edition retains its own source and selection. The reviewed-refresh workflow compares the selected records and meaning, displays complete change pages and exports, and records the operator's decision before creating a successor publication. A changed filter or time window remains visible in the comparison's scope.

The operator reviews column aliases, types, null handling, units, keys and source definitions before saving the query. The original dictionary carries applicable datetime types, codelists, units and URI definitions into the selected output. The saved semantic review binds the actual capture and reviewed projection. Publication uses the existing dataset publisher and seller release store; the [semantic operating workflow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/public-commons-sprint-2026/blob/f6c37fede046f2e234da5cd35421415f06969654/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf30-seller-sdk/ckan/SEMANTIC_WORKFLOW.md) follows that same source through merchant and customer receiving.

The merchant supplies its selected query resource, payment terms and original release. The existing Buyer receives the advertised offer, prepares the retained intent and destination, and continues its original Stellar request, payment and delivery workflow. The complete customer manifest, paged data and selected dictionary retain their source and snapshot identities. This connects the useful data service to the existing merchant and buyer operation.

The customer receives the full edition through the original analytical tools. Python/SQLite preserves the reviewed meaning beside immutable SQL editions, saved queries, CSV/JSONL/SQLite exports, reports and workbooks. DuckDB carries the same source meaning into its warehouse, physical tables and saved views. The [common analytical receiving guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/public-commons-sprint-2026/blob/38bcf07416c67e44f5be5c251cb3c4edaa803ba3/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/python/SEMANTICS.md) accompanies these maintained customer engines. The customer can query the delivered data, inspect its original dictionary and reopen a complete independently received workspace.

Staff use the [CKAN Support operation](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/496e1e1d2f69bc66bd6c3ef24c5d830c04ecc68c/products/ckan/SUPPORT.md) to select the retained publisher and inspect its actual source, publication and pending work. The original SF93 observer supplies the incident and recovery history; the existing SF74 desk retains acknowledgment, original details and complete case handoff. A received publisher keeps the original declaration, capture, publication and release identities through the existing relocation and installation bindings.

### Agent operation and received service

The same merchant agent can open the configured CKAN workspace, inspect an edition and dictionary, save a reviewed projection, create and review its original publication, export/stage its merchant package and receive full source, merchant and buyer files. Browser, CLI and agent operation use the same retained workbench and release store. Whole-project receiving retains its selected editions, publication history and original files before reopening the delivered service.
