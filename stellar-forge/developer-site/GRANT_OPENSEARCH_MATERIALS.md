## Search products, supplier catalogues and complete customer receiving

### Customer job and useful recurring output

A merchant with an authorised search index or supplier catalogue needs to turn that source into a dependable customer operation: a declared query, a useful complete result, reviewed material identities and quantities, and a delivery another operator can continue. The [complete material product](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/9ae97486f55905326bed06372d0094e0b9a18921/products/material-catalog/README.md) joins those tasks in one maintained workspace. A supplier's new catalogue edition can change the result of a recurring buyer request; the retained source, mapping, customer decision and query revision let the recipient understand that change and use the appropriate delivered edition.

This is a concrete source-and-receiving capability for the existing Stellar Discovery Trust Layer programme. The customer selects its original data rights, fields, query inputs, operating account, intended output and payment terms. The funded engineering applies this maintained capability to the selected independent merchant and buyer installations. Customer implementation, customer purchases and source-native development receiving keep their original financial and operational records.
