## Editable grant planning, amendments and delivery agenda

### Keep the working plan attached to its original operating case

A delivery lead needs to revise the work without losing the connection between the proposed plan, recorded delivery and actual payment deadlines. The [case planning workspace](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/25f629f5fd0d02efbe800b3db7d5160818bf09b6/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/CASE_PLANNING.md) provides that complete course inside the existing Planning & Operations application. Open an operating case and choose **Edit plan and workbook for this case**. The desk keeps the original case and pinned baseline beside the working native plan, complete scenario, source project, revision note and selected comparison date.

Each case continues to select an explicit saved planning revision. A working revision can therefore be reviewed without changing the operating baseline. The original amendment save, review and apply operation records the deliberate change, while preceding work, invoice, payment, receipt and review history remain available in the same case.

The planning draft is retained under the signed-in staff identity. Saved case planning work, complete draft download and reopening carry the entered inputs, original workbook bytes, source selections, notes and any unresolved original save. Switching to another retained draft requires preserving or explicitly discarding an unrelated unsaved plan. The original tab sequence check reports a concurrent update; current entered work remains available for download before reopening the saved local edition.

### Recover the original save and review the exact saved revision

Before saving, the case desk retains the full original mutation: plan identity, expected native revision, scenario patch, complete planning/source inputs, note, request ID and staff identity. If its response is lost, **Receive original save** sends that same request through the native transaction journal. The returned revision and content hash are saved before the pending request is cleared.

A confirmed revision conflict retains the whole entered draft for an explicit rebase and review. A later plan save by another operator remains a separate saved version. The displayed result and its native downloads continue to select the actual revision returned by the original save.

**Review saved revision in this case** returns that exact plan, revision, content hash, comparison date and note to the original [Plan amendments workflow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/25f629f5fd0d02efbe800b3db7d5160818bf09b6/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/AMENDMENTS.md). The complete [amendment calendar comparison](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/25f629f5fd0d02efbe800b3db7d5160818bf09b6/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/AMENDMENT_CALENDAR.md) preserves both current and proposed agendas, every changed and unchanged entry, original payment/form clocks, recorded assignments and the frozen amendment. ### Operate the selected cases together

An agenda source link opens the original cash, work package, receipt, tranche or case calendar. Completion receives its original receipt and package selections together with the reviewed date. An event's own due date remains separate from that review date.

A retained planning draft stays attached to its original case when the agenda opens another case. Returning to planning or its saved amendment uses that draft's own selected plan and date. The agenda keeps its own case selection and filters across reload. Desktop and phone use the same grouped Operations navigation.
