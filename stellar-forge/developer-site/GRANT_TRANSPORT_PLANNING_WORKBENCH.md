# Transport Planning Workbench — installation and customer operations

## Transport Planning Workbench: complete operating-source, performance and recipient delivery

Transport Planning Workbench joins an existing privately supplied transportation-planning application to Merchant Applications, the original browser and CLI, and a customer agent that retains complete commands, source files, reports and working handovers. The delivered unit is a reviewed, editable source-to-report workspace that another authorized recipient can independently receive and operate.

The generic public contribution supplies installation and transport code. Complete original application source, customer operating data, case correspondence and commercial terms remain privately supplied under a literal full-file authority. The integration retains the original Workspace, Operations, CSV intake, Node evaluator and archive readers. It adds no alternative transit calculation engine or competing dashboard.

This is an additive delivery capability within the existing canonical SCF programme. It creates no separate funding request or payment scheme. The current six-package, 400-hour, USD38,000, 150-day programme remains the funding and commercial authority. Implemented source, actual operated customer evidence and funded future commissioning are distinct records; the customer work order retains its own approved scope, price and acceptance.

### Customer outcome and complete workflow

Transportation planning needs the original operating records behind each measure and the reasoning behind a recommended action. A report alone may lose source corrections, units, missing coverage or the scenario assumptions used to prepare it. The workbench retains those relationships:

1. Receive complete authorized source and the actual original customer workspace.
2. Retain original documents and full CSV source families.
3. Review field mapping, original units, identifiers, timestamps and missing data.
4. Import original and corrected editions without replacing prior source.
5. Select governing service-rule editions and reporting periods.
6. Publish source-bound performance under the planner's explicit definitions.
7. Review cost scenarios and publish their complete assumptions and values.
8. Maintain accountable actions and dependency-complete implementation plans.
9. Produce source-linked report editions and separately record actual delivery events.
10. Export full working source/data/history, receive independently and cold reopen.
11. Record observed acceptance through existing Installation.

The planner retains method selection, service interpretation, recommendations and client responsibilities. An agent may prepare data and carry out selected native operations; it cannot convert an unreviewed source, missing coverage or a scenario into an observed customer outcome.

### Original-file intake and source correction

CSV intake accepts four original kinds: trip, coupon, cost and observation. The operator preserves the original headers and chooses native field mapping and explicit defaults. The parser retains source bytes, SHA, original row position and original identifiers. A source family selects its current predecessor before an additional edition is accepted. Identical source bytes plus kind, mapping and defaults can reuse the original native edition; changed inputs remain a distinct deliberate selection.

The original limits are 32MiB per CSV, 100 source columns and 100,000 input rows. A metric selection uses at most 100,000 rows across its chosen editions and only one edition from each source family. These are application limits, separate from an individual customer's smaller commissioned scope. The adapter does not impose the original private case's commercial row or family quantities on another customer.

Preview is deliberately bounded. It returns up to 40 rows and up to 100 errors, duplicate records or conflicts, together with relevant totals. Preview is not the complete dataset. Import retains the original source and saves all row/error identities in the native workspace. Datasets with errors remain marked for correction; the analysis selector requires validated editions. Agent record reads follow original offset/total pages of at most 1,000 rows.

Host-declared file inputs bind complete SHA, byte count, filename and media type before dispatch. Valid UTF-8 CSV is decoded without deleting a BOM or rewriting original numeric text. Document intake supplies original bytes as native base64, preserving the declared filename and media type. The original source store permits document bodies up to 64MiB, while the original text-only document alternative has its own 8MiB bound. Private connection, launch, source-authority and capability files are excluded from ordinary agent inputs.

### Units, populations and useful performance

The retained evaluator separates trip legs from round trips and coupon books from individual vouchers. Original rider namespaces prevent equal provider-local identifiers from being treated as one person across unrelated services. Dates, timestamps, service statuses and currency remain explicit. Operational quantities are whole-number counts; published observations and scenario assumptions may use the original bounded decimal-text format.

Performance is selected from actual validated source editions and an explicit reporting window. The native engine retains the governing evaluator, metric-definition and source/rule bindings. Duplicate records and conflicting revisions remain distinguishable. Unknown completion statuses or incomplete cost coverage must not become a supported cost-per-completed-unit assertion. Timing coverage and rider-key coverage are separate from overall record counts.

Published aggregate observations retain their own period labels, source notes and exact/minimum/maximum/approximate qualifiers. A historical public announcement does not establish a current operational baseline, current unique ridership or current cost per ride. Report consumers can inspect the full native result and its original source selections; abbreviated tool previews remain navigational.

### Governing service rules and reviewed scenarios

A service-rule edition records its programme, title, original references, observed date and any effective start/end dates. Service review explicitly selects one governing edition per programme, verifies its current revision and applicability at the chosen review date, and saves the full selected rules beside the evaluator result. A later change does not rewrite an earlier review.

Scenario cost lines keep programme, description, quantity, unit, unit cost in integer cents, fixed cost and the source or assumption behind them. The native decimal calculation preserves exact line arithmetic and its explicit cent-rounding rule. A scenario must be reviewed before it becomes a published scenario edition. Published editions retain the original scenario revision, approver and full assumptions. Scenario quantities are plans, not measurements or authorized spending.

This workflow gives the responsible planner a traceable calculation and an editable choice. It does not automatically recommend service cuts, eligibility policy, procurement or an expenditure. Customer and prime responsibilities remain those selected in the actual workshare.

### Actions, report editions and actual delivery meaning

Actions preserve owner, phase, due date, status, dependencies, source references, completion evidence, effort and cost assumptions. Dependencies must be valid and acyclic. A completed action requires an original completion reference. Publication of a plan includes every dependency needed by its selected actions, preventing a report from hiding prerequisite work.

Report sections contain the authored text and selected original references, including their full captured record values. Reports are immutable published editions. Their text remains the responsible author's interpretation; the source connection makes that interpretation reviewable.

The native delivery record records a selected published edition, authored status, destination, receipt text and time. Creating that record does not send a file, prove a recipient opened it or establish acceptance. Actual transport and recipient evidence are recorded only when observed through the existing customer workflow.

The original exporter supplies JSON, CSV, printable HTML and complete ZIP editions. HTML tables may show a readable bounded selection while the accompanying CSV retains all original exported rows. Review includes those accompanying files and their actual reopened content.

### Private source preparation and managed Applications

The Installation profile is transport-planning-workbench. It uses existing Workshare, Installation workorder, assignments, source selection, payment and acceptance. Its eleven steps bind the actual services, customer, period, data custodian, planner, source owners, host and recipient outcome. Existing integer atomic prices, counts, budgets, network, asset, recipient and spending decisions are preserved.

Preparation receives a complete authorized source directory and a literal external manifest. Every ordinary source member is verified by path, bytes and SHA; the executable delivery SOURCE_FILES declaration must match that full source selection. The original source has no third-party Python or npm dependency requirement. Preparation observes the selected Python3.12+ and Node18+ executables and imports the actual modules without constructing a customer Workspace or running a business analysis.

Private source transfer is separate from an operating application archive. The receiving host independently observes its selected executables; source-host executable paths are not an installed-runtime receipt on another host. Managed startup and the original CLI bind the actual selected Node through the native TRANSPORT_NODE selection. The adapter's own Merchant runtime uses its existing supported Node version independently.

Applications Review/Save/Start binds full private source, workspace, executable selections, actor and private launch/connection. The bridge starts the actual original loopback server and serves the unchanged original user interface after establishing private browser access. Named staff profiles remain original attribution; they are not invented access roles. Stop joins the actual listener and handlers before releasing the original owner. Saved SQLite source, history and command records remain available after reopen.

Commands are transport-planning-workbench, transport-planning-workbench-prepare, transport-planning-workbench-managed and transport-planning-workbench-receive. Distribution composes existing grant-workspace, Merchant provider tools, browser assets and shared Applications infrastructure. Complete declared support includes every adapter, private-runtime preparer and receiving source; private application bodies are not placed in the public package. [G1–G8]

### Complete customer-agent operation

The transportPlanning provider registers 21 tools. They cover configured attachments; connect/disconnect; full installation info; original browser opening; original state/catalogue/record reads; literal and host-file CSV preview; native command and full JSON command input; complete CSV/document intake; original independent receiving; full artifacts; retained files, byte ranges and JSON inspection; and complete selected request observations.

Each of the twelve original commands preserves request.id, command, complete args and the host-selected actor. Before HTTP dispatch, the adapter retains that full selection and any complete source file. The original Workspace takes BEGIN IMMEDIATE, checks its saved command identity, applies the operation, audits it and saves the entire result in the same transaction. An identical request/actor returns the saved result without overwriting later edits. Different content under the same ID conflicts. The existing native journal remains the authority; the adapter adds no competing mutation engine.

All observed HTTP responses, including errors, are retained in full. A successful same-request result can be reused. An explicit same-request retry after an error can use the native command journal; response loss does not change the request ID or payload. The transport label HTTP_OBSERVED describes that response only. Completed body custody can finish after the listener closes without requiring a second live network request.

Retained file and request listing filters the complete current workspace/source/actor authority before pagination. Reusing an alias for another workspace cannot expose the older private records. Later file reads reauthorize the current original installation. Whole artifacts are streamed, counted and hashed; the established range reader returns complete content through EOF. JSON pointer inspection is a 32MiB navigation facility, not a truncation of the underlying file.

### Complete receiving and editable handover

The native application archive contains every original selected application file and, when included, a complete workspace recovery ZIP. Recovery contains a SQLite snapshot with full original sources, dataset rows, configuration, record history, events and committed commands. Original archive readers check the complete member inventory, bytes and hashes, database integrity, workspace identity, row counts and source hashes.

Application receiving installs source and optional workspace into a new separate destination. Recovery receiving restores the original snapshot into its own new workspace. Both retain the original staging, hashing and sibling-lock semantics. These are whole-directory operations, separate from the transactional command journal. An uncertain acknowledgement preserves the same archive and destination for actual inspection; the adapter must not invent a fresh target or declare an error to mean no publication occurred.

The installed receiving course extends the original equipped programme. It adopts the actual original result when available, preserves every historical source edition and full archive, uses the unchanged readers, operates browser and agent access, retains exact command replay, independently receives complete onward delivery and cold reopens it. Technical local edits used during receiving are labelled and do not fabricate customer performance.

No source text alone asserts that an unrun native course succeeded. Customer readiness requires the actual original application, all outgoing files and recipient opening route to be operated and personally viewed. The original sender and the person responsible for customer acceptance remain unchanged.

### Recurring work and measurable acceptance

A useful recurring unit is a newly approved operating period, service-rule revision or scoped scenario/report edition with complete receiving and review. Downloading ranges, retrying a command, copying an archive or cold reopening an existing edition is part of that edition. Such operations do not create extra sales, supplier counts or paid acceptance events.

Acceptance records the actual source and executable identities; reviewed original workspace; complete input and report populations; command replay preserving later edits; full native archive restoration; editable independent receiving; and personally viewed desktop/narrow controls, attachments and reopened exports. Source review, a manifest match, HTTP200 or a screenshot that nobody viewed does not replace this outcome.
