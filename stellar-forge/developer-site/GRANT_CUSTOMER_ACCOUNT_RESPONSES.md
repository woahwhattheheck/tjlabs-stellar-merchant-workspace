### Customer account delivery, original responses and continuing collections

The installed merchant can publish one customer's complete dated account and receive their invoice-specific response through the same accounting workspace. The account preserves the original invoice and credit documents, customer-specific cash and allocation history, refunds, corrections and source files. Its account date and file identities are fixed when published. The complete delivery includes an offline customer desk that opens the original documents and retains messages, payment promises, invoice disputes and reported-payment references. The customer downloads a complete response file for the merchant's established contact channel.

Staff receive that original UTF-8 file against its saved publication, customer and workspace. Each response entry is reviewed beside its published invoice and the current invoice at the selected effective date. A changed account requires explicit acknowledgment; the original current-balance limits still govern a promise or dispute. An operator chooses the reasoned outcome and, where appropriate, explicitly reopens a case, replaces its earlier promise or resolves its recorded dispute. The saved decision and original Collections events commit together under the existing accounting writer. A reported payment remains source for matching through the actual receipt, bank, remittance or Stellar collection workflow.

The full staff flow supports publication, original-file intake, entry review, saved forms and continued request recovery. The customer package and internal review archive remain separate complete outputs. A following customer response keeps its own identity and previous-response reference. Another operator receives the whole accounting backup with its retained exports, reopens the original account and response, and continues the next entry or collection activity with its saved version and request history.

This product is available through the existing browser, typed HTTP client and native `customer-account` command. The same MerchantAccounting connection provides eight account/response operations, the original request lookup, both complete retained-file families and the typed remote client. Its durable `merchant-accounting-receivables` command saves the complete preview before preparing a write; original source text, operator choices and request identity survive execution and reconciliation. Configured source aliases resolve once during preview, and the resulting saved operation retains the exact text.
