## Recurring economic-report installation

The maintained economic application supports recurring analyst briefings and complete county reports in one saved project. Staff retain original source editions, review the selected reporting period and calculation profile, produce the useful report, publish its original files through the existing merchant, and receive the paid result through the original Buyer. Release watches and series vintage histories drive later analyst acquisitions; county profiles retain their selected geography, industries, reporting quarter and price basis. A second engineer can receive the complete economic project, reopen its saved plans and reports, and continue the same reporting procedure.

**Useful customer task and roles.** An economic research desk, reporting team or software application needs a defined report for a later period while retaining the basis of its previous publication. The commissioning workorder names the organization paying for implementation, the merchant controlling the source and publication, the independent buyer using the result, and the maintainer accepting the operating handover. It selects the source rights and access, series or county/industry coverage, calculation choices, reporting interval, required output, native payment terms and receiving application. The independent operator assesses the finished task by opening the whole delivered report and tracing its measures to retained current and baseline observations or original county source rows.

**The paid unit.** A published analyst edition offers its complete offline `briefing.html` with local measures, source views and CSV/JSON exports, or the corresponding `briefing.json`. A published county edition offers one complete `report-package.json.gz`: the selected report, every original predecessor edition, complete manifests, original HTML/PDF/JSON/CSV, methodology and predecessor comparisons. The customer can receive that package in its own directory, reopen any included edition and download the original files. Current county report files are also available individually. Each advertised resource identifies its exact byte count, SHA-256 and content type. The buyer prepares that GET and reviews the original network, asset, recipient, integer atomic amount and authorization lifetime. The original Stellar SDK, facilitator and Buyer perform verification, settlement, retained output and recovery. Each selected resource keeps its original request and price; the installation workorder specifies the agreed complete output and aggregate spend.

**Acquire the right source edition.** A saved release watch names the series or original briefing template, observation interval, baseline date and whether to catch up every applicable vintage or select the latest available one. The operator retains the complete official response pages and reviews calendar changes, series vintage dates, coverage and source meaning before saving the plan. Calendar dates describe the publisher’s release schedule; the returned series vintage dates drive actual observation acquisitions. An incomplete source interval exposes the exact additional selection to acquire. The operator then selects a planned date, and the original FRED observation provider acquires its exact series, period and as-of selection into the same economic project.

**Board-to-FRED migration and current-publication reporting.** The same economic project retains the original Federal Reserve Board package, official series crosswalk, complete source catalogue and saved migration. The analyst reviews the selected source series, frequencies, periods and mapped FRED series before running the complete current-publication programme. Its Current publications desk acquires all mapped publications for that saved plan or attaches an already completed programme. A retained request, progress and original output let the operator continue the same job after a closed browser or restarted host.

The analyst opens each original series and its full comparison rows, with exact decimal strings, source scales, period labels, missing codes and original-file locations. The six result categories distinguish equal values, changed values, unavailable values on both sides, changed availability, Board-only periods and FRED-only periods. Retrieval timestamps describe the current public tables; the original API release/vintage workflow retains its separate as-of selection. A later collection creates another retained edition while the earlier report remains available.

**Complete publication delivery and independent receiving.** The selected edition carries its original programme inputs, full source manifest and transports, all comparison rows, HTML report, native project and complete downloadable archive. Large originals stream to the receiver; the browser pages the original row file without replacing the complete result with a sample. The receiving engineer opens the delivered native project, attaches the retained programme and continues from the same saved migration and source population. The [installed receiving guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/355e98f3ea45ccc322d2a6a8651e522f14ab3bec/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf30-seller-sdk/fred/ddp/PUBLISHED-WORKSPACE.md) covers the browser, CLI, typed client and HTTP/MCP operation.

**County source and report production.** A saved profile selects the reporting geography, industry comparisons, original reporting quarter and price basis. The operator imports the complete retained BLS QCEW intake and original CPI capture into the same project, produces the full report and retains its source availability and disclosure/missing-value meaning. Later editions keep their original predecessor, report files and complete comparison. The merchant publishes the saved report package, so the customer receives the same useful files reviewed by the operator.

**A recurring edition.** The template retains the original series/comparison selection, relative-change choices, precision and meaning. Its calendar policy names the number of complete months, the selected-series observation-date rule and any trailing months to omit. Missing markers retain their original dates; a quarterly observation date remains its source period label. Preview exposes every selected series’ source and window coverage, changed units/frequency/seasonal adjustment, new or departing periods, and changes to current values and baselines. Saving a successor retains the normal analyst review and complete report, ready for the original publication and customer path. The full predecessor comparison is also available as HTML, CSV and JSON.

Reopening a saved report or resuming its download retains the original purchase. A later reporting window or newly retained source edition begins the next useful operation: review the selected acquisition, inspect the changed report and approve its publication. Catch-up completion belongs to the selected watch and exact release snapshot, so a later snapshot can reveal an additional series revision on a date already processed. Previous plans and buyer reports remain readable with their original source and values. Source retrieval time, API real-time/vintage dates and observation-period labels retain their distinct meanings.

**Retain the complete customer operation when the project moves.** County customer receiving saves its original destination with the same purchase before writing report files. A complete project handover now carries the merchant publication, every prepared request, completed or pending Buyer reference, and each original customer receiving selection. A receiving engineer can relocate the economic project, reopen an unstarted request with its original preparation, or recover a completed purchase through the same configured Buyer. The transferred customer inventory preserves the original request, configuration, report, payment and selected receiving path; the Customer desk reopens every current and predecessor report file from that retained operation.

The receiving host keeps the original Buyer store and customer receiving directory available through their existing backup and restore procedures. A separately downloaded complete package can instead be received into another directory through the installed command. Publishing and project transfer reuse the original compressed report bytes. The current project extension includes full customer state; earlier publication-only exports remain readable with the information they originally contained. Receiving a project restores its saved requests and selections, and the operator uses the normal review and execution controls to continue them.

**Complete county operating programme.** The maintained source-to-customer programme acquires the original county intake and CPI capture, produces the complete retained reports, operates the desktop and phone workspace, and continues through the native Merchant and Buyer. Its paid phase selects the original report and every predecessor, purchases each current advertised resource, recovers the original complete-package request after a closed browser, receives the original report archive independently, and transfers the complete economic project. The final receiving phase reopens both the prepared and completed customer requests in the relocated project and downloads every current and predecessor member. The programme preserves its original selections, payment results and full operating output between continuations. The county programme is delivered to the original operating team; its completed result will join this same customer example.
