## Audit Exchange: original building audits, energy review and editable turnover

Audit Exchange gives an engineering team one project for the original building audit, official validation, corrections, source-bound findings, energy analysis and contractor handover. Its useful customer output is a reviewed audit edition with the complete source and an editable receiving package. The commissioning firm can keep that package current as equipment information, findings and reporting periods change.

A changed workspace requires a current preview; a lost response recovers the original request and its accepted result. Removing a return from a later queue does not erase the earlier return or its history. A readable index lets the receiving engineer open the original manuscripts and their review records.
