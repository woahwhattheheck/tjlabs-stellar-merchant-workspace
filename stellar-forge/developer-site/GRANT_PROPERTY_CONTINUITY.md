## Property continuity: full source reconciliation, rehearsals, cutover and independent receiving

A property migration is useful only when the receiving operator can explain what happened to the complete agreed population, its relationships and its controlled records. Matching a total or importing a file does not establish that the right property, space, lease, document version, project or financial amount reached the intended platform. The existing Property Continuity application gives the commissioning partner a saved working case for reviewing those decisions, recording two rehearsals and an authorized cutover, and handing the complete editable result to another operator.

The [original operator guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/commons/blob/a64e69c1bc6cb30685865e49ef98e2428b29f6e8/opportunities/bluewave_property_continuity_20261013/OPERATING.md) defines the local workflow. It retains original source bytes, attributed mapping reviews, independently acquired target returns, every discrepancy and its disposition, actual load observations, and the receiving history. Its company-specific starting point is the issued IDA property-management requirements used by the original Bluewave workshare. That requirements analysis supplies a concrete integration context; it does not establish an appointment, purchase, permission to access a tenant or successful commissioning.

### Start with the customer's complete working cohort

The workorder names the customer, commissioning partner, original source custodians, approved platform writer, mapping reviewer, cutover authority and independent receiving operator. They agree the complete cohort, its effective cutoff and timezone, expected denominators, source and target platforms, required related populations, controlled-document custody, correction policy, and the useful output the customer will receive. An explicit excluded population remains outside that acceptance claim.

The original application is a Python and SQLite workspace with a browser and command-line interface. Its source, host and original workspace remain under the selected operator's control. The grant chapter preserves that existing application; an installed Applications/provider connection, paid resource or agent interface is a separate selected integration until its actual implementation and receiving evidence are supplied. The local application does not write to Salesforce, Docusign CLM, Centros, ArcGIS or a customer tenant.

These are reviewed domain responsibilities, not guessed vendor object names or a promised connector contract. The partner must supply its actual supported export and load interfaces. Tenant credentials, permission to mutate, production accounting and signatures stay with their original authorities.

The operator imports complete original UTF-8 CSV, JSON or JSONL files as source editions. An acquisition records whether it is SOURCE or TARGET, the platform and family, original extraction identity, time, method and reference, effective cutoff and population scope. Files remain downloadable without rewriting their bytes. A source acquisition precedes the observed load; a target return must come from a separate actual acquisition after that load and bind its original operation.

A FULL declaration requires a known denominator that agrees with every observed record. A partial selection, change feed, unknown denominator or count mismatch remains explicit. Where completeness is not established, the application does not turn an unmatched row into a confident missing/extra conclusion. A legitimately empty complete population has an explicit zero denominator. Distinct hashes alone do not prove independent acquisition, and independently acquired unchanged files may legitimately have identical hashes.

### Map identity and meaning before comparing the return

The [complete domain contract](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/commons/blob/a64e69c1bc6cb30685865e49ef98e2428b29f6e8/opportunities/bluewave_property_continuity_20261013/CONTINUITY_DOMAIN.md) requires reviewed mapping editions. Composite keys retain their original text, integer or boolean types. Explicit platform and identity namespaces distinguish equally spelled identifiers from different populations. A later mapping correction creates a new edition and its own attributed approval or rejection.

Field rules distinguish exact typed values, text, decimal strings, offset date/time and booleans, including required and null semantics. CSV retains strings; it does not infer numeric or boolean meaning from a convenient spelling. Empty, absent and null values remain different. Monetary values use exact decimal strings with a separate currency. There is no inferred exchange rate. Original JSON non-integer tokens and unsafe integers remain exact; browser/report descriptors carry their token locations so JavaScript display cannot silently round the original evidence.

The operator records the actual approved writer's load identity, input digest, original reference, start/completion observations and SUCCESS, PARTIAL, FAILED or UNKNOWN outcome. This is an observation of another authority's work. Saving it does not issue a transaction or platform request, and a transport acknowledgment does not become successful business posting.

A reconciliation selects the exact approved mapping and complete source/target pairs. It reports MATCHED, MISSING, EXTRA, CONFLICT, DUPLICATE and UNVERIFIED with original row locations and complete field differences. Duplicate members remain visible rather than choosing a convenient winner. Cross-population checks retain property and space references, hierarchy cycles, document-version identity, currency and decimal meaning, CRS/units and active-lease intervals. A date alone cannot make a nonactive lease active.

A named reviewer may accept a reviewable discrepancy with its reason and original evidence. That decision preserves the discrepancy; it does not rewrite it as a match. Coverage, ambiguous identity, invalid types, hierarchy and overlapping active-lease blockers require correction and a later reconciliation. The customer can therefore distinguish a correct result, an explicitly accepted business exception and an unresolved condition.

### Carry the same case through rehearsal, release and handover

The first rehearsal records its selected reconciliation and actual outcome. Acceptance requires resolved nonwaivable blockers and a current explicit disposition for every remaining reviewable discrepancy. Rejected outcomes stay in the case.

The second rehearsal uses a later actual acquisition of the same complete populations and a distinct actual load operation. Corrected mapping editions must descend from the original reviewed mappings. An unchanged independent later acquisition is permitted; omitting difficult datasets is not. The second rehearsal preserves its link to the first and retains the later decisions.

The cutover plan freezes both accepted rehearsals, the selected mapping editions, cutoff and authorized change window. It names the authority, continuing-write or delta policy, and rollback or forward-correction procedure with the actual available recovery evidence. Release authorization rechecks current mapping approvals and discrepancy dispositions. The plan's original release basis remains historical even when the final returned population resolves an earlier issue.

Accepted final receiving requires the complete actual cutover return and the current applicable approvals within the authorized conditions. A later rejection of a selected mapping prevents accepted receiving. A missing return, failed load or overrun can be recorded as rejection after the window, retaining known observations and leaving unknown times or identifiers unknown. It does not fabricate the recovery result or a completed target population.

Editable handover follows accepted cutover and records the receiving operator, actual reopening and later correction references. The complete working case retains the source, mappings, commands, original first results, findings, decisions and publication history. Another engineer can reopen it and add an attributed correction without erasing the original acceptance basis. Business acceptance remains a named customer's decision; local application execution alone cannot supply it.
