## Property continuity: full source reconciliation, rehearsals, cutover and independent receiving

A property migration is useful only when the receiving operator can explain what happened to the complete agreed population, its relationships and its controlled records. Matching a total or importing a file does not establish that the right property, space, lease, document version, project or financial amount reached the intended platform. The existing Property Continuity application gives the commissioning partner a saved working case for reviewing those decisions, recording two rehearsals and an authorized cutover, and handing the complete editable result to another operator.

The [original operator guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/commons/blob/a64e69c1bc6cb30685865e49ef98e2428b29f6e8/opportunities/bluewave_property_continuity_20261013/OPERATING.md) defines the local workflow. It retains original source bytes, attributed mapping reviews, independently acquired target returns, every discrepancy and its disposition, actual load observations, and the receiving history. Its company-specific starting point is the issued IDA property-management requirements used by the original Bluewave workshare. That requirements analysis supplies a concrete integration context; it does not establish an appointment, purchase, permission to access a tenant or successful commissioning.
