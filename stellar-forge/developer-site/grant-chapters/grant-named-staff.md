## Named grant delivery, contributor work and complete staff handover

The delivery team needs to assign responsibility, review completed work, reconcile supplier invoices and continue the project when an operator changes. The implemented [named-staff workspace](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/b412f1ea76bd3f243c2ef78eeb4360730b65ef80/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/STAFF_ACCESS.md) performs those tasks against the original Grant Operations plans, cases, attachments and financial records. It supplies the project's operating foundation for the two installations selected in the grant plan and its six planned work packages.

### One operating case from contribution to payment

An installation administrator enrolls each person with a lead, finance, reviewer or contributor role and selects that person's actual cases. A contributor's profile binds to their original contributor record in each assigned case. Staff use individual sign-in codes; access changes take effect on their next request and end their existing sessions.

|Person|Complete operating task|
|  ---  |  ---  |
|Contributor|Retain original work files, record completed work against a package, submit it for review, enter a multi-line invoice and inspect its paid, open and credit balances.|
|Reviewer|Read the submitted work and attachments, accept or reopen the same record, and prepare the original implementation, cohort and tranche-completion reviews.|
|Finance|Review or dispute the invoice, record an observed cash movement, allocate it to the original invoice and apply an explicit correction when needed.|
|Delivery lead|Maintain the assigned operating case, coordinate work and customer handovers, and use its current planning and review functions.|
|Installation administrator|Manage staff assignments, reissue or revoke access, inspect the original history and export the complete installation.|


The browser and HTTP service apply the same role and case selections. Each business event retains the enrolled person's stable recording identity. A later display-name or assignment change preserves that history.

Contributors can retain a draft while another person updates the case. If the same work or invoice changes, the desk presents its current saved version beside the retained draft for an explicit decision. An interrupted save keeps the original request identity so receiving the result continues the same transaction. These behaviors let the team work concurrently while preserving each work record, invoice and attachment.

### Carry contributor preparation from agreement to delivery

The Contributor engagements desk brings each named person's actual terms into the same operating case. An operator prepares a complete agreement book containing roles and scope, availability dates, seven weekday hour values, dated exceptions, compensation and payment terms, remaining package allocations, original supporting sources and the recorded agreement decision. A blank availability value remains unstated; an entered zero records no availability on that day. Original decimal values remain intact throughout the review.

The book distinguishes a proposed engagement from a confirmed, declined or withdrawn one. A confirmation retains its actual decision maker and original time, with a timezone. The operator reviews the complete book and saves an immutable edition against the original native preview and case version. A later change to availability, terms or acceptance becomes a sourced successor that retains the earlier edition.

This agreement record connects the proposed delivery team's capacity to the work it can accept. The operator selects confirmed agreements from one saved book and a saved Forecast with the same review date, reviews the affected packages' remaining hours, costs and payment choices, and maps the named contributors to the original Portfolio resources. An existing Portfolio input retains its complete calendar, tasks, alternatives, notes and Installation phase selections. The original Forecast and Portfolio engines calculate the dated cash and allocation results.

The handoff saves the reviewed Forecast first, presents the allocation for review, then saves the selected Portfolio and the receiving record joining both outputs to their source book. The recorded allocation status and ready alternatives remain available when the lead chooses a delivery plan. Original customer conditions, observed cash and commercial work stay attached to those native records.

### Preserve the complete preparation between working sessions

A delivery lead may need to pause while collecting an agreement, correcting a dated exception or reviewing a resource allocation. **Contributor engagements → Working copies** keeps that unfinished preparation alongside its original case and recording-staff scope. A named checkpoint retains a complete point in the work: all entered agreement fields, the selected saved book, original previews, handoff choices, receiving stage, complete original commands and receipts, visible selections and unfinished add-row controls. When an operator selected a complete Portfolio file, the working copy includes its original bytes, filename, size and SHA-256 together with the retained native input.

The operator can capture another named checkpoint without replacing an earlier one, inspect a saved checkpoint, download the current preparation or inspect a downloaded working file. Inspection presents the original and current case, plan, staff binding and source metadata, the complete retained commands and results, and the receiving stage before any selected state becomes active. Full agreement fields, handoff choices, unfinished controls and original Portfolio bytes remain available in the complete file and explicit data views.

Opening is a separate choice. **Reconcile and open this copy** preserves a recovery checkpoint of the active preparation, checks the original commands against the native journal and reopens the saved records by their actual identities before adopting the selected state. An already accepted operation continues from its original result. A retained request without a result stays available for the original explicit resume control. Unsent previews are reviewed again when the case has changed.

The complete preparation and named checkpoints are saved in browser storage for the original installation, case and recording-staff scope. A save compares the version first opened, so a second tab's newer work remains visible as a conflict. Required retention completes before a native command is sent. Large original Portfolio files remain part of the complete preparation, and **Download current preparation** remains available from every tab when retention needs attention.

These controls let an operator preserve a partially completed cash or allocation handoff and return to the same source book, Forecast, Portfolio and receiving step. They also keep the current preparation downloadable when local retention needs attention. A selected checkpoint can be removed through an explicit local removal control while native books, attachments and financial history remain in the operating case.

An existing installation can recover its earlier browser preparation through the same inspection pane. The desk retains the whole original saved text and key, stages the known agreement fields and request history, and identifies provenance or file bytes the earlier format did not retain. The operator explicitly reconciles and opens that preparation. Earlier saved draft files can be inspected through the same reader, while their complete retained original data remains in the working file.

### Reopen the preparation with its complete operating case

The portable working file carries the entered preparation and original request context. The existing complete engagement-case export carries the original attachments, Operations history, saved agreement books, Forecasts, Portfolios and receiving joins. A receiving maintainer uses both when continuing the whole operating case, and inspects the working file against its original case and staff binding before opening it.

The operator guide follows that course through named capture, inspection, download, reopening and explicit native continuation. The same source edition is received through the original Contributor operator, with its existing case, staff identity and original files. The maintained browser, native command and export paths therefore remain one connected delivery workflow from agreement preparation through dated planning and independent handover.

Within the grant programme, T1 uses this preparation to record the actual delivery contributors, their agreed availability and the work allocated to them. T2 uses the retained native receiving stage to continue integration work as sources or planning inputs change. T3 carries the complete preparation, original attachments and saved operating history into the selected installations' handover. These activities use the existing prospective package allocations and agreed staffing inputs.

### Review actual Work, corrections and remaining effort

The [Installation effort desk](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/226b40e26e01bdeb0e4ac997bfe9522589594055/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/INSTALLATION_EFFORT.md) reads the complete source phases and original Work records for a saved Installation and a selected review date. The lead assigns selected hours from a native Work record to its original phase. One work record can be split across phases or current Installations; the report displays the other retained allocations and the unassigned balance before more hours are added. Each allocation stays tied to the selected version of the original record.

Recorded Work continues through its normal submission and acceptance process. If a contributor corrects the underlying hours or source, the Installation retains the earlier attribution and identifies the changed record. The operator can reduce an allocation while resolving that correction, explicitly select the corrected record, review the affected phase estimate and save the next complete edition. The case keeps the original correction history, invoices, funding basis, attachments and customer observations.

For every phase, the reviewer enters the effort still required and records who reviewed it, when, why and which original source supports it. An entered zero means the phase has no remaining work under that review; an unentered estimate remains visible. The report shows quoted effort, recorded and accepted Work, reviewed remaining hours and projected total effort together. It identifies allocations beyond the available Work quantity, changed or void records and date spans that require review. A total recorded over several dates retains its complete interval, so an operator can resolve a review-date boundary using the original source.

Support is reviewed against the customer's actual terms. Select the original plan wording, included hours, covered phases, acceptance or handoff event and calendar period. The saved edition retains that source plan and the selected wording; a changed plan calls for an explicit review of the new terms. An accepted observation supplies the actual support anchor in its recorded timezone. The report distinguishes work inside, outside or spanning the included period, retained allowance and projected overrun.

The existing Freeform scope illustrates six included support hours within fourteen calendar days after acceptance. Query.Farm retains eight hours across thirty calendar days after complete handoff. Lutra's six-hour operator handover is delivery work, with its two remote sessions retained under their own original terms. These differences stay attached to the respective workshares when the team reviews support capacity and future work.
