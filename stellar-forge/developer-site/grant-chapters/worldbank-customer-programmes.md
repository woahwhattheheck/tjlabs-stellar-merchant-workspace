## World Bank economic programmes and complete customer delivery

A research service can deliver a customer's economic brief as a continuing, editable application. The maintained World Bank product carries original country, source and indicator selection into complete acquisition, analyst review, an optional dataset merchant, saved SQL/report/workbook work and independent customer receiving. The customer keeps the accepted source and its definitions while a later approved edition is reviewed against the customer's current work. This supplies a concrete merchant-resource and handover use case for the existing SCF build.

### The customer job and its source meaning

The commissioning brief selects the question, countries or aggregates, indicators, source database, periods, reviewer and useful outputs. A regional research team might maintain a country comparison or a recurring economic briefing; those are service scopes an operator can configure in the product. The customer chooses report sections, worksheet names and labels, missing-value display, recurrence and acceptance. Commercial terms and actual customer outcomes are recorded through their original Workshare and Installation, with the complete source attached.

The source engine retains every original page, request/response metadata and source definition. It separates country identifiers from ISO labels, marks aggregates, preserves period/unit/status/footnote metadata, and retains original numeric tokens as text. A null stays missing. Repeated observation identities require an explicit original-row choice or exclusion before export. Source acquisition, a saved analyst review and the resulting customer edition have their own identities.

That distinction matters in ordinary reporting. Excluding an observation from an unchanged provider capture is an analyst decision. A later capture may contain no changed observations. The programme exposes source-selection, review and provider-metadata changes alongside complete observation differences, so the operator can explain why the new customer edition differs. The implemented source contract is documented in [the original World Bank workspace](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/8baf462fb821f91c8fc2fc7f48db66a8be1c89ab/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf30-seller-sdk/worldbank/README.md).

### One saved customer programme

| Customer stage | Implemented operation and result |
| --- | --- |
| Acquire and review | Retain the complete selected source; compare captures; save original row choices; export an approved portable handover |
| Establish the first report | Validate that handover through its original importer; reproduce the reviewed CSV; create typed source documents, saved queries, report and workbook |
| Receive the next approved edition | Retain its original ZIP through the resumable intake and register the exact source/document/revision in the running Analysis owner |
| Compare current customer work | Bind the programme to the actual view/report/workbook; review every observation difference and original metadata/review change while the previous view remains selected |
| Accept and refresh | Apply the explicit review digest/note; run the reviewed query/report revisions; refresh related worksheet sources while preserving current titles, notes, labels and unrelated sheets |
| Deliver and receive | Transfer reports, CSVs, workbook handover, original comparison/selection and complete editable workspace; reopen source and programme history in the receiving installation |
| Continue after restore | Verify the accepted source/report/workbook and create an explicitly linked portable delivery that includes current customer edits |

The recurring engine preserves the customer's selected SQL revisions and report parameters. Saving a newer SQL query alone does not silently change a report that pins an older revision. A concurrent view, report or workbook edit is handled through the original revision checks. Removed or repeated source columns expose the complete worksheet proposal for an operator's explicit mapping choice. Prior report publications, workbook definitions and source decisions remain available.

The analytical template makes its calculations readable. Original observations and coverage retain exact source values. Annual change is calculated only for consecutive four-digit years with matching source, country, indicator, aggregate and unit; gaps, duplicate periods, missing values and zero prior denominators yield explanatory uncalculated results. When GDP in current US dollars and population are both present, the additional section calculates their matched ratio using positive population. Derived values use the existing SQLite numeric rules and numeric spreadsheet cells; original tokens use text cells. These are the defined report recipes, which the customer can edit and review, rather than an implied statistical interpretation of every World Bank series.

### Merchant operation and Stellar relevance

The optional dataset service uses the existing publisher, version/release store and Buyer. An operator supplies the actual merchant origin, route, network, asset, atomic amount and recipient; the buyer reviews the actual offer, binds its intent and carries explicit spending/page limits. Retained original source files travel with the reviewed dataset release. This reuses the grant's existing Stellar merchant and customer-acquisition architecture for a useful economic-data service.

Source acquisition and a local report refresh do not themselves make a purchase. The original World Bank reference's challenge-only merchant preparation is distinct from the application's existing completed team-operated testnet baseline. This contribution leaves the baseline at 18 purchases and 0.0411 XLM, fees separately recorded, and leaves independent customer adoption as the funded deployment objective.

The installed Merchant adapter can operate the original source/browser model and connect the already running Analysis programme. The existing Console workflow series carries a period through source preparation, a review pause, publication and complete file retention. It preserves the native operation, workflow calls, review digest and full downloadable output. An uncertain response is reconciled through its original identity. These controls let an operator or agent continue the same customer work without launching a second database owner.

### What the recipient keeps

The customer receives HTML/JSON reports, complete section CSVs, Excel, an independently receivable workbook package, the source comparison and selection, and the complete Analysis workspace with its manifest. The approved original source ZIP remains downloadable after workspace relocation. Repeating the same completed archive receive preserves later customer edits.

A restored snapshot may show the old outer ZIP assembly as interrupted. The programme separately verifies the accepted source selection, report publication and completed workbook. It can package those accepted outputs into a new linked delivery with the current workspace snapshot, retaining the older assembly and every accepted predecessor. Browser, CLI and Merchant use the original review, download and archive contracts.

The original installed source/merchant journey completed on October 12: the saved application imported and reviewed all 42 CAN/MEX/USA GDP/population observations, published the edition, started its challenge-only testnet merchant, downloaded all six customer files, reopened the same capture/export and independently received all 42 rows. The [original completed receiving record](https://tokenjunkielabs.slack.com/archives/C0BTB4SUCP9/p1791785852097869?thread_ts=1791764726.590409) identifies the complete installed source and retained customer handover. This establishes the source/merchant foundation of the customer programme.

The maintained original course selects the retained 42-observation CAN/MEX/USA GDP/population handover and an approved 41-row review from the same capture. It covers customer changes, full comparison, original registration, refresh and portable receiving. The original equipped receiver records actual run and acceptance outcomes. The registered implementation and its source-native course give the grant a complete, inspectable product capability; each installed customer returns its own commissioning and use records.

### Fit within the funded build

This capability contributes to the existing six packages without adding hours or changing the request. Merchant-resource integration can commission the customer's selected economic source and useful paid-data route. Authority and handover can give an independent operator the complete accepted source and editable report. Provider/ledger deployment and deployed threat response retain their existing facilitator, release, spending and recovery work. Mainnet rollout and production recovery carry the same selected customer service into its real deployment and receiving environment.

The original proposal remains USD38,000 equivalent, 400 prospective hours and 150 days from actual award acceptance. Day30 retains two independent merchants and second-operator handover; day90 retains two buyers, at least ten useful testnet purchases across seven consecutive days, two facilitators and two installed response cases; day150 retains two mainnet merchants, two buyers, at least thirty useful purchases across fourteen consecutive days, returning buyers and receiving-engineer restore/reconciliation. Choosing an economic-report customer uses this existing allocation and measurement scheme. Original purchase, customer and award outcomes remain attached to their actual records.
