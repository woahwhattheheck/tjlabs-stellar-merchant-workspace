## World Bank customer programmes and complete analytical delivery

A recurring economic-report customer needs the later source edition applied to its own saved analysis, report and workbook, while preserving prior publications and customer edits. The installed World Bank programme supplies that workflow inside the original Analysis application. A named programme retains the customer's view, report, workbook, observation comparison keys and original source selection; staff review a candidate edition before refreshing the agreed output.

The [complete customer operating guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/8baf462fb821f91c8fc2fc7f48db66a8be1c89ab/products/worldbank/PROGRAMMES.md) starts from the customer's existing workspace and native World Bank handover. The commissioning workorder names the source, countries, indicators, reporting periods, desired report, interpretation owner and receiving engineer. Where access is sold, the merchant's advertised resource, buyer intent and agreed aggregate spending stay with the original purchase. Preparing a programme or reusing an acquired edition is customer work; it does not create another purchase.

### Review a complete source edition in the customer's application

The World Bank tab uses the same private Analysis workspace, capability and saved sources as the existing SQL, report and workbook editors. Its source picker pages through retained document revisions, including older editions, and shows each original handover identity, capture context and reviewed observation count. A programme saves the actual existing customer definitions as an immutable revision with its original request identity.

Preparing an edition calls the original World Bank engine and compares the candidate's typed observations against the customer's current view. The review retains the complete comparison, original numeric tokens and NULLs, provider metadata, source/review identities and accepted report/workbook definitions. Metadata changes and observation changes keep separate meanings. Excluding an observation from an unchanged provider capture is a customer review change; it is not described as a later provider update.

The operator supplies the exact review digest and a source-selection note before refresh. The original recurring engine selects that reviewed comparison, runs the customer's saved report and refreshes the related workbook sheets. Unrelated worksheets retain their original sources. If columns changed, the operator reviews the full affected column mapping, including positions, types, NULL handling and precision. A customer edit after review is exposed by the existing revision checks, so the current definitions receive a new review. The [Analysis programme guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/8baf462fb821f91c8fc2fc7f48db66a8be1c89ab/products/worldbank/ANALYSIS_PROGRAMMES.md), [saved programme coordinator](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/8baf462fb821f91c8fc2fc7f48db66a8be1c89ab/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/python/stellar_forge_buyer/worldbank_programmes.py) and [original analytical engine](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/8baf462fb821f91c8fc2fc7f48db66a8be1c89ab/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/python/stellar_forge_buyer/worldbank_analysis.py) preserve that chain from source to accepted output.

### Continue the original operation after interruption

Programme revisions, complete request bodies, source reviews and operation history live in the original SQLite workspace and travel with its portable archive. The browser retains a request before submitting it. Reopening the tab reads the same operation; continuation uses its original ID and frozen inputs. Completed jobs retain their original results. A failed job that cannot resume has a distinct retained attempt under the same business operation.

The coordinator sequences existing Analysis jobs and drains before the workspace releases its original lease. The installed `starforge worldbank-product programme` command exposes the same saved programmes, sources, reviews, history and delivery operations. Neither route substitutes a demonstration report for the customer's edited definitions.

### Deliver the accepted edition and an editable customer workspace

The accepted output includes the complete original report, workbook, source handover, comparison exports, workspace archive and customer delivery ZIP. Downloads verify the retained file descriptors and stream the complete files. A receiving engineer reopens them through the original workspace and workbook receivers.

A workspace snapshot made while its own outer delivery is being assembled can retain that assembly as interrupted. The restored application keeps this historical state and separately verifies the already accepted source selection, report publication and workbook. It can prepare a new portable delivery linked to those accepted results without acquiring the source again, recalculating the report or overwriting a workbook definition. The new workspace snapshot includes later customer edits; the selected report and workbook remain the original accepted editions. Original and later delivery histories remain distinct.
