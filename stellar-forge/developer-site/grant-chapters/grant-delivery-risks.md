## Delivery risks, accountable mitigation and complete project handover

A delivery lead needs to keep an identified problem, its supporting material, the responsible people and the next action together as a project changes. Grant Operations now provides that workflow inside the existing customer case. The lead records an assessment, assigns or explicitly leaves an owner unassigned, reviews mitigation work and saves a dated edition. Preparation can be retained as a complete editable working copy without submitting it. Two saved editions can then be compared with their original assessments, dated reports and supporting context kept together. A receiving operator can reopen the original case with every earlier assessment and the files that supported it, including the exact comparison selected for handover. The [complete Delivery risks operating guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/46ac7e1f6b1c03075766ec2217deea8fb7bc791a/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/RISK_REGISTER_OPERATOR.md) describes the Delivery risks desk and its ordinary staff roles.

This is a project management tool for the proposed merchant installations and customer handovers. The operator supplies the actual assessment and decides the work. Its reports make recorded ownership, dates, source references and follow-up visible alongside the existing plan. They do not issue delivery restrictions, approve a deployment or change a budget, forecast, payment or contributor commitment.

### Record a useful assessment in the existing case

The owner or assigned lead opens Customers and delivery → Delivery risks in the original Operations workspace. A native seed supplies the complete current register, the case contributors, the saved work packages and the retained source choices. The operator names the register and selects its assessment date. A first edition may be empty; an entry is added when a person identifies an actual risk or an issue that has already occurred.

Each entry has a stable identity, title, description, category and identification date. It retains the selected contributor or an explicit unassigned owner, the owner's context, the recorded state, likelihood, impact, priority, reasoning, trigger and next review. An issue uses the native Not applicable likelihood. The [original risk engine](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/25b25073ccf65c17287d002e814a50f73abda5e0/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations_risks.py) checks the complete document and its predecessor; a risk may become an issue, while its original identity and identification date remain available.

The assessment uses explicit categories selected by the operator. It does not manufacture a probability, expected monetary loss or revised forecast from a missing value. The native report shows the whole edition's status counts, unassigned owners, overdue reviews, overdue actions and source or work-package findings. An older edition is evaluated at its saved assessment date, so reopening it does not recast its report as today's assessment.

The entry links to the original saved work packages and supporting sources. The operator retains an attachment through the case's existing source workflow, then chooses that original source in the register and records the relevant context. A historical package link remains visible when a plan later changes. New links are checked against the actual selected plan and case. The full source identity accompanying each edition makes a later change to contributors, packages or sources visible without rewriting the earlier assessment.

### Give mitigation work an accountable owner and outcome

Actions belong to the risk or issue they address. Each has its own stable identity, title, scope, selected contributor or explicit unassigned owner, ownership context, state, due date, progress note and supporting sources. Planned and In progress work remains visible until the operator records Done or Cancelled with the required date and outcome. Future due dates and next-review dates are permitted; recorded identification, review, completion and resolution dates must be consistent with the assessment date.

For example, a participating merchant may discover that its selected source cannot yet be used with the intended authority, or that a receiving operator has not completed the agreed recovery exercise. The responsible lead records the observed condition and source, links it to the appropriate installation or handover package, and describes the concrete mitigation. The record can identify an owner, an evidence request and the next review without representing that person as contracted or their availability as confirmed. The example becomes a real case entry only when the operator supplies the actual information.

A changed inherited entry carries a dated review, reviewer and note. Closing a risk or resolving an issue retains a resolution date and evidence. Existing entry and action IDs are preserved across successors; completed or cancelled work remains in the lineage. This lets a customer see what changed, who recorded the review and what material supported the outcome.

The [complete browser desk](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations_risks.mjs) preserves the working draft while the operator opens historical editions, supporting files and entry history. Filters help inspect an owner, kind or state while the native totals continue to describe the entire selected edition. The complete normalized document and native report remain available for review before saving.

### Keep the complete preparation and original request portable

A working copy carries the editable desk before the next native edition is saved. It retains the full draft, selected entry and filters, recording note, current and historical review observations, preview, original pending or known accepted command, and complete request observations. The complete [current browser implementation](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations_risks.mjs) keeps this preparation in the original case and signed-in staff scope. The working file does not grant another person the original staff role or turn an imported receipt into a native result.

The operator downloads the whole working-copy JSON, or stages an earlier complete desk file for inspection. Reading the file displays its identity, draft, retained request and checkpoint history before any editable state is replaced. Explicit reopening first reads the original case, current register, selected review and original request table. It verifies the complete pending command or accepted result against those native records. If another unfinished command already belongs to the open desk, that command must be resolved before a different copy can replace it.

A successful reopen retains both the preceding complete desk and the selected imported original as checkpoints. Their full contents are available to review and download. The native acceptance is recovered when it exists; a genuinely unfinished command keeps the same identity and body for the operator's later explicit recovery. Imported absence, rejection or success claims cannot authorize a new save. The reopened draft needs a fresh preview against the current case before an operator records another edition.

The portable envelope has a checksum for its complete payload. That checksum detects changed bytes; it does not establish the truth of an assessment or replace the native source and request checks. The supported file limit is 32 MiB and the desk carries at most sixteen complete, unnested checkpoints. If a reopen would exceed that inventory, the existing copies remain available; the operator can download and deliberately remove an older local checkpoint before proceeding. Removal changes the local checkpoint inventory, not an accepted native edition.

This working file is for continuation in the same case and staff scope. A colleague who needs a complete independently received case uses the native case ZIP described below. An editable document can also be exported through the existing draft-document control and reviewed in the appropriate native case. These three materials—working preparation, editable document and complete native case—retain their own identities and purpose.

### Save an edition and recover the same request

Preview uses the original Operations case version, current register head, selected source and complete draft. Editing the document or observing a new case version invalidates the old preview. The owner or lead supplies a nonempty recording note and explicitly saves the reviewed edition through the original command writer.

The desk retains the complete command before dispatch: original request ID, expected case version, recording operator, action, normalized document, preview identity and note. Its read-only request lookup first checks that same command. A matching accepted result reopens the existing edition; the workflow retains an uncertain request until it can reconcile the original outcome. A known success is retained before the following case and edition reads, so a later display or local-storage failure can recover the saved edition without creating another one.

A stale draft remains available for review. The native current-head and case-version checks prevent it from silently superseding work another operator has saved. Returning a definitely rejected request to a draft requires the explicit original rejection and a subsequent lookup showing no accepted result. The operator then selects the current source, previews and deliberately submits a new request.

Owners and leads may save within their permitted open cases. Assigned finance and reviewer staff may inspect, seed and preview; their existing role does not permit the new save command. Contributor staff retain their current desk. Entry ownership is an assignment within the register and grants no extra application permission.

The [native CLI](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations_risks_cli.py) and [typed client implementation](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/agent-client.mjs) use that same case and writer. The [complete agent operating guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/AGENT.md) carries the request, preview, source-input, snapshot and export interfaces through the existing Merchant provider. A browser, CLI or authorized agent therefore operates the same original register, rather than maintaining a separate risk database.

### Compare two saved assessments with their original context

The lead opens Compare saved editions and chooses both complete original reviews. Each side is bound by its review ID and SHA-256 value. The [native comparison engine](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations_risk_comparison.py) reads and validates both original reviews from one current case snapshot. Comparing an edition with itself, an earlier edition with a later one, or reversing the pair produces an explicit direction while preserving each original record.

The result includes every risk and mitigation action: added, removed, changed and unchanged. Stable risk/action identities make the relationship inspectable. A field difference distinguishes a missing field from a field explicitly set to null. The comparison includes both complete saved reviews, their assessment dates, source selections and native reports, so the operator can open the original evidence rather than relying on a summary row.

Recorded risk fields, the dated report and linked context are shown separately. A later assessment date can change whether a review or action is overdue even when its recorded description is unchanged. A renamed contributor, changed work package or different source can change the surrounding context without an operator editing the risk itself. Separate row markers and filters preserve these distinctions. Count differences report the two saved editions' actual native counts; they are not a new progress score, probability, financial estimate or declaration that a risk improved.

The [comparison desk](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations_risk_comparison.mjs) keeps the edition pair and filters with the original staff/case session. It pages the complete risk, action and context rows and can show unchanged or context-only records. Opening either full original review uses the existing desk. Source downloads are offered against an actually matching current case source identity; the browser checks the complete downloaded bytes and hash. Historical source references remain visible even when a different current source cannot stand in for them.

The comparison is read-only. It creates no new register, review, request or case version. An operator who decides to change an assessment returns to the original draft, preview and permitted save flow. Finance and reviewer staff retain their original inspection permissions. The [case integration](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations.py) and [authenticated HTTP interface](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations_http.py) preserve those existing transaction, source and staff boundaries.

The native result has a comparison SHA-256 that binds its complete content. Browser and agent consumers retain that native value rather than recalculate it using a different JSON serializer. The complete JSON download contains the comparison itself; a readable HTML report and separate risk, action and context CSV files carry the same selection. The operator can share the appropriate reading material together with the complete case when the recipient needs to continue the work.

### Hand the complete assessment to another operator

Every saved register is an immutable original case review. Entry history follows its stable ID across all recorded editions, including earlier actions, dates and resolution evidence. Reading an older review shows its original document, report and source selection alongside the current case context.

The selected-edition export is the original complete Operations case ZIP. It includes the case's original sources and history plus the selected risk JSON, two CSV views and readable HTML. The next operator can inspect the readable views and receive the full native archive through the existing case importer. The importer validates register lineage and source bindings while retaining original review identities. Current risk history follows the recorded edition version, independent of the array order used in the portable archive.

A selected comparison can be handed over with that complete original case. Its ZIP contains the exact selection, complete comparison JSON, both original reviews, readable report and three CSV views. Export binds the native comparison digest and the observed current case version, so a later change to the case requires the operator to select its current state deliberately. The smaller JSON, HTML and CSV downloads bind the comparison digest without imposing the case-ZIP version condition.

The importer first validates the original case, sources, review lineage and event history, then regenerates all eight selected comparison members from those validated saved reviews. It checks the complete regenerated bytes before the import mutates the receiving case. The saved producer review identities remain original even though native case receiving has its own current case identity. The returned selectedRiskComparison is passed through the [original Operations opening flow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/be3410549846b3cb55074f5d83ee242592e73676/stellar/scf_2026/sf47_budget_model/workspace/operations.mjs); the Risk desk resolves any already accepted save, verifies both original review identities and the native comparison digest, and opens that exact pair. A failed read leaves the selected opening available for retry.

### Apply the tool within the proposed funded work

The existing grant basis remains six prospective packages, 400 estimated hours, USD 38,000 and 150 days. The original rate assumption and unconfirmed contributor allocation retain their current meaning. The risk register supports planning and handover within those packages; it adds no separate reserve, fee, staffing promise or automatic allocation.

| Existing delivery package | Useful operator-managed risk and mitigation work |
| --- | --- |
| Merchant resource integration | Record the actual source authority, schema, deployment and connection issues discovered during a participating merchant's installation, together with the owner and original evidence needed to resolve them. |
| Merchant authority handover | Review named operator access and agreed handover work; retain the observed recovery or role-transfer result and its receiving materials. |
| Provider and ledger deployment | Follow actual provider cursor, version or authentication differences and the original operation identity through their assigned integration work. |
| Deployed threat response | Record deployment-specific findings and the responsible mitigation; link the real alert, acknowledgment and recovery evidence when that exercise is performed. |
| Mainnet merchant rollout | Track the participating deployment's identified issues, explicit release or rollback work and the operator's dated findings. Existing transaction and release authority remains in its original workflow. |
| Production recovery and handover | Preserve the actual restoration and maintainer findings, completion of agreed corrective work and the complete case passed to the receiving operator. |

Already merged reusable implementation belongs to the pre-award foundation. Any further completed pre-award work is reconciled against remaining scope before an award; the current package values are not retrospectively described as payment for this implementation.
