## Stellar price books, customer values and complete downstream receiving

An application using Stellar asset prices needs a repeatable customer operation: select the exact assets and quantities, collect their original observations, decide which observations meet the saved quality policy, publish a reviewed edition, and receive those values into the same downstream application. The Rumble customer workspace performs that whole course while retaining the supplier's original responses. A later collection produces a comparable edition; the customer can identify quantity changes, price changes, missing coverage and changed observation times before publishing.

The existing source workspace retains asset discovery, watchlists, current and historical collections, original request/result inspection, price-age and source-quality review, pacing and saved quota continuation. Its customer book names native XLM, exact case-sensitive classic asset identifiers or Soroban contracts. Quantities and prices stay decimal strings; fixed-point arithmetic preserves their full multiplication and sum. The saved book selects USD or XLM and its explicit age/carried/unknown-price policy. The original supplier's observation time `as_of` and snapshot refresh time `updated_at` remain separate. An unpriced observation or supplier zero-price sentinel has no usable value. When any position is unavailable, the complete total remains unavailable while the available subtotal and reasons remain visible.

### The customer's recurring task

| Operator step | Saved result and customer use |
| --- | --- |
| Select the existing customer and edit its asset book | Exact quantities, denomination and quality rules receive a new book revision; the prior published edition remains attached to its own original book. |
| Plan and collect prices for that saved book | The maintained provider runner records the complete plan, actual request attempts, original responses and one retained source run. |
| Receive an edition and inspect changes | Every configured asset appears. Quantity effects and price effects reconcile exactly to the value change; newly missing and newly covered assets remain explicit. |
| Review and publish the chosen edition | The original revision check records reviewer, decision, note, complete book and selected source. An interrupted edition request resumes with its same operation ID and input. |
| Deliver JSON/CSV to the receiving application | A persistent wallet receiver keeps complete prior feeds, adopts a newer customer revision and reconciles an identical repeated feed. Receiving an older first-seen edition retains history while the current view stays on the newer revision. |
| Receive a later source and hand over the product | The complete source/workspace export retains plans, original response bytes, books, immutable editions, reviews and downstream files. Another host reopens the same customer identity with the delivered source. |

The original team-operated customer completed this course on October 13, 2026. It retained its existing three-asset book, received a later actual provider collection, changed quantities in the browser and added a fourth asset from the actual catalogue. Receiving the previous collection against that expanded book recorded `NOT_REQUESTED` for the added asset and an unavailable complete total. The operator then collected the full expanded book, reviewed and published its fourth immutable edition, delivered the values into the same wallet and repeated the original receiving. The final customer revision was 9 and book revision 2. All four positions had values; the exact computed USD total was `3433488.48159080132517383340231482746201`. This total is the configured demonstration book's calculation.

The complete 83-file, 600,540-byte source/customer handoff independently reopened with its delivered source, reproducing the exact published feed and wallet replay. Five desktop/phone screens completed with no browser page or local HTTP errors; the 390-pixel phone viewport retained a 390-pixel document width. Thirteen independently computed decimal position checks, plus the totals, matched. The full original receiving course, source responses and screenshots remain together in the saved original case.
