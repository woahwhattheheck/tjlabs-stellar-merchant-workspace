## Service recovery that reaches the buyer’s books

### The customer outcome

A buyer needs the useful service it purchased and a correct record of what happened when delivery failed. The merchant may recover the output, agree a partial return or identify a remaining correction. The buyer then needs the credit to reach the right expense item, reporting period and accountant handoff. Keeping those links saves staff from repeatedly matching support tickets, payment records, exported files and bookkeeping rows.

The Service-to-buyer operation carries that work through one saved customer case. The merchant selects its Service report and explicit monetary basis. Its configured accounting operator reviews the adjustment and the returned-transfer evidence. The buyer receives the complete capture, codes the original purchase and credit, resolves period issues and issues the period. The accountant’s reviewed return comes back to those original rows. A receiving engineer can reopen the continued project with its source, decisions and customer files.

### Architecture and operator control

The existing merchant workspace owns the Service terms, report, proposal, prepared intent and native outcome. The configured accounting workspace verifies the selected payment and adjustment and exports the original purchase, linked credit and complete adjustment statuses. Buyer Expenses owns source intake, exact native-unit allocations, issue dispositions and issued periods. The Accountant workspace owns its handoff and permitted returned fields.

Each step uses the configured owner’s existing workspace and request journal. Before dispatch, the operator reviews the exact input and original workspace selection. When a response is interrupted, recovery reuses the retained request and body; an already committed result is returned before applying a current-version check. Appending the outcome preserves other operators’ intervening case work. The complete export carries the applications, buyer state, issued period, accountant files and original requests together.

The monetary decision stays explicit: the Service report supplies the observed event and reviewed basis; the responsible operator authorizes the adjustment. The original purchase, adjustment status, returned-transfer proof and buyer coding remain separately inspectable throughout receiving.

### Continue the staff work and receive a complete draft

The saved Buyer workbench groups every prepared action, original outcome, period, delivery and accountant return by the selected case capture, configured Buyer alias and actual native workspace. Staff reopen a selected workflow, inspect its complete original records, use the saved references and review the actual current native state before the next decision. All action pages and full prepared-request/outcome JSON remain available. An action saved in the case may still need its original outcome recovered; the workbench retains that request’s identity.

The browser keeps the entire editable draft: selected action, actor, references, exact allocation strings, every label row, incomplete fields and the native edit bases originally loaded. It saves only that draft’s unsubmitted work. A field can be incomplete while staff are preparing it; the original native preview remains responsible for deciding whether a financial action is ready.

To hand that work to another browser, staff download the complete draft JSON, select the original case and Buyer workspace on the receiving origin, and choose **Review draft file**. Review validates the full field set and the exact case, capture, configured alias and native workspace. It shows the incoming draft before receipt. The operator then chooses **Receive reviewed draft**, after saving or resolving any intervening edits in the receiving form.

One browser-store transaction retains the complete uploaded file, its name and byte identity, the prior draft, the received draft and the receipt history, while replacing only the matching current draft. That replacement checks the same predecessor reviewed by the operator. The receipt can later supply the complete original file or previous draft. Reopening restores the original choices and edit bases; preparation then reads the native state through the existing owner. The original prepared and pending native requests remain in their own unchanged request stores.
