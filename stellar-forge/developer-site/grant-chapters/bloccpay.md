The Bloccpay commissioning application connects an existing customer payroll and invoice ledger to its selected Stellar Disbursement Platform (SDP), Horizon and Bridge records. Its complete operator includes programme mapping, original payroll import, exact provider requests, signed-event intake, reconciliation, exception decisions, period reports and independent source/runtime/workspace receiving.
