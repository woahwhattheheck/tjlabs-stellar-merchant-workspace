## Haystack building models: reviewed point lists, validation and contractor handover

A building integrator receives point lists whose identifiers, names, units and equipment descriptions were authored by different people or systems. A contractor may correct names and relationships and return a later model while the receiving engineer has continued working. The useful output is the complete reviewed model, its original source associations, native validation evidence and an editable handover that preserves both parties' work. A changed label, duplicate heading or interrupted report must not silently change the point's identity or erase the source behind a decision.

The maintained Haystack product now supplies that workflow alongside its original typed-grid and corrective-work application. It imports complete original CSV/TSV files or selected native Haystack grids, previews explicit column and naming choices, saves full semantic model versions, and prepares native Brick models and contractor handovers. The actual standard is the original Brick 1.4.4 distribution with its included REC vocabulary. Node serves the workspace, browser, HTTP/CLI/MCP operations and durable request history; the selected offline Python environment supplies the original CSV reader and Brick/RDFLib/pySHACL engine. Building records, model files and private customer evidence remain off-chain.
