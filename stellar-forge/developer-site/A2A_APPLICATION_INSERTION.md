### Customer commissioning calendar and complete useful jobs

The fourteen-day mainnet target needs an explicit customer calendar. On an ordinary Monday–Friday schedule without holidays, two buyers making one useful purchase each per business day produce twenty purchases in fourteen calendar days. The proposed commissioning calendar reaches thirty through fifteen independently useful jobs per buyer: ten complete source-period extracts and five separate reconciliation or reporting decisions. Both proposed merchant installations serve both buyers. The customer workorder must replace the relative dates, source periods and roles with actual customer selections before this plan can be used.

The [complete customer commissioning specification](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/33dfb64798f5316a586c165d4d7591de972ca4e2/stellar-forge/developer-site/A2A_CUSTOMER_COMMISSIONING.md) and [thirty-job register](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/33dfb64798f5316a586c165d4d7591de972ca4e2/stellar-forge/developer-site/a2a-customer-commissioning.json) name each job's purpose, period, complete output and receiving role. Buyer A's additional jobs cover new or missing observations, late corrections, asset coverage, metadata/status changes and a correction decision register. Buyer B's additional jobs deliver distinct two-period briefs, a source-quality publication, a complete first-workweek report, a selected-cohort report and a complete two-workweek publication. These are prospective customer adaptations to approved saved queries/reports; customer identities, procedure IDs, source licenses, prices, signers and receiving people remain unselected.

For each actual job, the receiving record joins the original customer approval, priced request, native settlement, A2A task, merchant request, full output byte identities and SF82 release/edition to the named receiver's decision. Useful customer jobs, priced requests, native settlements and complete received tasks are counted separately. Retries, reconnects, resumed work, several formats for one result and unnecessary repeat purchases do not create additional useful jobs. The existing development reference and team testnet baseline remain separately attributed.

The appendix preserves the existing $38,000-equivalent request, 400-hour allocation, 150-day programme and milestone definitions. Its remaining customer work fits once into the existing six funded packages, or into a separately commissioned workorder where applicable. Already completed reusable source is the selected starting component. The current manuscript and installation owners approve the actual calendar and accountabilities; this prospective plan records no executed customer outcome or payment.
