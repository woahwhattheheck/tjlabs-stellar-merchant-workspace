## CanadaBuys qualification, bid decisions and complete customer receiving

A supplier or procurement adviser can commission a working qualification desk around the tender editions it actually uses. Staff retain the official notices, the company's capability statement, solicitation documents, supplier evidence and reviewed requirements in the same workspace. The result is a saved Bid, No bid, Investigate or Park decision with a usable report, named next actions and an editable handover for the receiving team.

### Carry a real procurement decision through the whole workflow

First import the governing official source and select the actual notice. Its original bilingual row, reference and solicitation numbers, closing field, linked documents and retained editions remain available. Save the company's capabilities, commodity-code families, delivery regions, qualifications, supporting documents, preparation time and commercial preferences as an explicit profile. **Check profile fit** exposes the original notice value and profile revision behind each match, gap or unknown.

Retain the solicitation, amendments and supplier evidence with their actual file bodies or recorded source/access state. Each assessment selects the exact saved document edition and page or section. A requirement retains its original clause, classification, applicability, source evidence, supplier evidence, reviewer and assessment. Later document changes remain visible alongside the edition used for the original review.

The operator selects the notice edition and company profile, records one of the four decisions, and uses **Review selected context** before saving. The model binds the saved decision to the complete reviewed context within the same transaction. A source, profile, evidence or requirement change calls for a fresh context review. Named actions retain stable identities, owners, due times and their open, done or cancelled state.

**Compare saved decision with current records** shows complete before and after values and the reasons for reassessment. A later comparison leaves the original decision available. Selected HTML, JSON and CSV reports carry the reviewed decision, source fields, profile, fit findings, requirements, evidence references, actions and changes. The standalone HTML is the reading copy; the full workspace package is the editable receiving material.

This gives the receiving adviser the source needed to continue the procurement work.
