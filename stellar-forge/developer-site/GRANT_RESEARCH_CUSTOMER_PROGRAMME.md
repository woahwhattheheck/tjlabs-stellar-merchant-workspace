## Research quotations, paid delivery and complete customer programmes

A research agency needs a repeatable way to quote an evidence service, perform the work, deliver a useful result and continue with the customer's corrections. The implemented Research product keeps that complete operation in one customer programme. Its [quotation workspace](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4cd7288a061bb8943619804f8211916f881e7c28/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/customer-quotes/OPERATING.md) joins the original commercial case to observed supplier costs and pricing, a reviewed Research brief, source-linked customer work, a frozen delivery and the realized delivery report. The analyst and customer continue the same case and dossier histories as the service changes.

### Commission and price the actual service

The [Applications commissioning workflow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4cd7288a061bb8943619804f8211916f881e7c28/products/research/COMMISSIONING.md) selects the real case store, Research workspace, pricing observations, delivery host and existing source connections. Preparation retains those complete selections for review; saving produces the original launch and Applications settings. The managed application can start, stop and reopen that same workspace. A selected source or configuration change returns to the maintained review path.

Within the customer case, the operator prepares the research brief from the agreed quote and chooses the actual dossier, project and source revision. The customer-response stage records the requested continuation against those originals. Quotation and pricing remain editable native commercial work; the programme records each complete request and displayed review before applying the selected action. This connects the agreed scope and price to the work that is actually delivered.

### Publish the selected result and receive the whole customer work

The [paid publication and Buyer workflow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4cd7288a061bb8943619804f8211916f881e7c28/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/customer-commerce/OPERATING.md) freezes the selected Research delivery before registering it with the existing merchant. It retains the exact original publication, customer handoff and archive. The Buyer uses its configured account, approval and execution flow to retrieve that selected delivery, then receives the original dossier through the maintained Research receiver. Cancellation and lost-response recovery stay attached to that same original purchase and receiving operation.

The [complete programme and receiving workflow](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4cd7288a061bb8943619804f8211916f881e7c28/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/research-dossiers/customer-quotes/PROGRAMME.md) carries every selected quotation operation, original phase input, native pricing and supplier-cost file, complete commercial case, source-bearing dossier, work snapshot and all four report formats. A completed paid publication contributes its exact published files and archive to that handover. The receiver reviews the original case transfer against the selected destination revision and retains the complete source package before applying it. The customer's received case and dossier are editable, with the commercial, service and Research histories needed for subsequent work.

The original request identities and review decisions remain useful after interruption or later peer edits. Completed requests reopen their saved result; continued work uses the received destination identities. The full file roster and original semantic receivers validate the selected package, including large native case records.

### Operate the same programme across the customer's tools

The browser, [installed Python interface and receiving example](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4cd7288a061bb8943619804f8211916f881e7c28/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/sf88-operator-buyer-cli/python/RESEARCH-QUOTE-PROGRAMMES.md), [Merchant operations](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4cd7288a061bb8943619804f8211916f881e7c28/stellar-forge/mcp-merchant-operator/RESEARCH_PROGRAMMES.md) and [installed remote terminal](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/4cd7288a061bb8943619804f8211916f881e7c28/stellar-forge/mcp-merchant-operator/RESEARCH_PROGRAMME_TERMINAL.md) use these same native engines. An analyst can work in the browser, another operator can continue a reviewed step from Python or the terminal, and an agent can operate the retained programme through the Merchant interface.

The remote terminal selects the original source and customer connections, retains complete input files, submits or resumes the existing jobs, exports every selected handover file and transfers the archive in exact resumable chunks. The receiving operator imports that whole archive, reviews the destination selection, receives the original case and dossier, and reopens them for the next service edition. Accepted cross-workspace work drains during Stop; interrupted file and upload operations continue from their original retained selections.
