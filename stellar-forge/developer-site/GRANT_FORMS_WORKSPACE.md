## Forms Workspace: complete captured-source mapping, original reconciliation and customer receiving

### Delivered product and customer problem

The Forms Workspace gives an implementation partner an inspectable, editable path from original form-system exports to a complete source-bound migration review. It receives entire selected originals, keeps the operator's field/key/attachment interpretation, runs the maintained source-native calculators, carries following editions without replacing earlier decisions, and gives the recipient complete source, data, results and working history for independent reopening.

A typical customer has several kinds of evidence that must remain distinct: form definitions, settings and dependencies, exported responses, original upload bodies, workflow histories and downstream receipts. A table comparison alone cannot establish whether an attachment belongs to the intended response, whether a historical field was omitted from an export, whether a workflow export is complete, or whether a target-side live operation was performed. The workspace represents these conditions explicitly and keeps their original evidence.

The implemented source is additive to the original discovery and migration engines. Its inner Forms project contains the complete unchanged application selection; the outer editable workspace includes the collector, native state, authoring interface, complete operator and API guides, native/browser receiving courses, and read-only Customer Installation bridge. The exact current product and all source bodies are selected by the matched registration accompanying this chapter. Public source contains generic software and explicitly invented operating examples. Customer exports, tokens, correspondence and commercial documents remain in their original authorized private cases.

### Complete source selection and six-class scope

The operator selects a full cohort and records include, exclude or defer plus the actual authority/reference for each of six classes:

| Class | Complete retained source | Computation and remaining operating judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Definitions | Original selected form definition bodies, IDs and revisions | Whole-file comparison and explicit tabular interpretation retain exact originals. Matching bytes or mapped fields do not establish that every rendered form branch behaves correctly. |
| Settings | Selected authentication, theme, dependency, notification and connector evidence supplied through the actual permitted source route | Original files remain inspectable with exact hashes. Account entitlement, private credentials and live behavior require their actual owner evidence. |
| Responses | Full selected original and returned-target exports, including explicit population/filters and original table interpretation | All selected records participate in exact keyed comparison. Missing or invalid keys, ambiguous matches, source-only/target-only rows and field differences remain visible individually. |
| Uploads | Original bodies, media/filename metadata and explicitly selected parent/form/response/field/repeat references | Complete bytes, lengths and hashes are verified. Actual parent references are checked against the retained selected side/table/record. Missing/redacted bodies remain gaps, not invented empty files. |
| Workflow | Supplied original definitions and authorized actual run/step/assignment/decision/history exports | Complete selected originals and known population limits remain visible. A restricted completed-work CSV is not silently converted into all in-flight or historical work. |
| Downstream | Supplied original request/result/receipt and destination identifiers | Source evidence and explicit comparisons remain separate from any live replay, side effect, notification, payment or customer acceptance. |

Presence and completeness are independent. PRESENT means a selected original body was actually received and its exact identity is known. COMPLETE, PARTIAL, REDACTED and UNASSESSED retain the author's completeness interpretation. MISSING and REDACTED declarations can identify unavailable original assets without supplying fabricated bytes. A genuine zero-byte original remains a present source with its actual empty-file identity.

Coverage is tracked for every selected asset. One compared CSV or file cannot conceal another unused source in the same class. Unlinked upload bodies, excluded classes, unknown historical coverage and unresolved parent claims remain explicit findings through the full export.
