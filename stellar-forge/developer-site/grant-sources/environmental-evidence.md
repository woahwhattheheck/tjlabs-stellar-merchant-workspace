## How the effort was selected

For Geotech, Waterborne, McCampbell and Phoenix, the original selection maps each phase to the corresponding numeric minutes in the retained Workshare estimate and divides those minutes by 60. It checks the phase sum against the plan's complete capacity total. KISTERS' unchanged phase descriptions explicitly assign 8, 14, 12, 8 and 8 hours. Its supporting effort document preserves those exact paragraphs and maps their numeric values to the original five phases and 50-hour total. No price-to-hours conversion, equal allocation or inferred staffing is used.

The adjacent `environmental-evidence.json` supplies every phase ID, original effort pointer, plan and packet identity, and the complete arithmetic inputs for this table. The application owner retains the corresponding complete private plans and receiving packages. Reviewers can read the published operating course below without access to the private workspace.

## From a proposed plan to a useful installation

Open **Installations → Prepare original source workshares** in the existing authorized Grant Operations case. Receive the complete selection and original files; review each phase and source value; save the reviewed selection; then prepare and attach the complete native supplier package through the original case writer. For each selected workshare, identify its actual merchant and independent buyer, authorized source, receiving engineer, host and approved useful-task spending. Preview and save that Installation through its original editor.

Carry the selected product through its complete source, review and output workflow. Export the whole operating case, import it into an independent workspace, and reopen the same selected Installation revision. The receiving engineer records the actual source, output and customer decision. The [complete installed environmental guide](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/6d18ad532aef87aad8982da1ff3c3426655ae014/stellar/scf_2026/sf49_data_commissioning/installation-workorders/ENVIRONMENTAL_INSTALLATIONS.md) provides the maintained commands, source checks, original request recovery and full receiving course.

| Evidence stage | Required record | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Source prepared | Complete source-preparation receipt and all outputs | The original plans and source bindings were prepared |
| Installation received | Native save, whole-case export/import and exact-revision reopen | The installation record was received in the original operating system |
| Product operated | Actual selected source/job result and complete verified outputs | The useful workflow ran within its stated source and environment |
| Customer accepted | Customer decision tied to the original scope and delivered output | The agreed work was accepted |

The complete source and installed profiles are published. This summary creates no later execution or acceptance claim. Any future native preparation, case receiving, customer import, paid job or acceptance is recorded from its actual result under the original owner.

## Funding and reviewer use

Use the table to understand the customer-specific work that surrounds the reusable product: source agreement, complete intake, explicit review, usable output, corrections and independent handover. The funded proposal selects its own two installations and assigns their prospective work once to the six existing grant packages. Separate commercial implementation milestones stay in their original workorders. An implementation fee neither sets the price of a laboratory or water-quality job nor authorizes its payment.

When reviewing a claimed outcome, select the exact original case and plan, identify the retained source and receiving result, and match the stated evidence stage. A generated format fixture or prepared profile alone cannot establish an accepted client import. The same distinction governs the grant's independent buyers, later useful purchases and original observation windows.
