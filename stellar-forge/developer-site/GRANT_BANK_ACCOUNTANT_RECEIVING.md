The [complete archive and financial-lineage reader](https://github.com/woahwhattheheck/tjlabs-stellar-merchant-workspace/blob/e7da0a7f1aff44d44398498063df2d2d9e97684b/stellar/scf-starforge-20261009/merchant-settlement-accounting/receivables/bank-statements/accountant-receiving/core.mjs) checks the complete Bank manifest and every original member's length and SHA-256, then checks the nested customer manifests and retained documents.
